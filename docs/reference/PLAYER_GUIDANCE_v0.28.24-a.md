# Player Guidance Reference — v0.28.24-a

Project Flint intentionally separates progression hints from recipe lookup.

- **Advancements:** short one-sentence mini-guides that tell the player what progression step to attempt next.
- **Recipe Guide:** exact workstation inputs/outputs for Baked Kiln, Mortar Kiln, Clay Grinder, Wooden Grinder, and Advanced Kiln.
- **Access:** press **K** in-world, or click the small book icon integrated into Clay Grinder, Wooden Grinder, or Advanced Kiln screens.
- **Survival inventory:** Minecraft's vanilla recipe-book button remains hidden and disabled. Do not expose Project Flint processing recipes through it.
- **Permanent Campfire:** intentionally omitted from recipe-guide station browsing; its use is taught through progression guidance and later kilns supersede its cooking role.
