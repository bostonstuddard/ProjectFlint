# Player Guidance Reference — v0.28.22-a

## Advancement eras

### Tool Era
Starts immediately and guides the player from Loose Rocks through primitive fire, Crude tools, Flint production, and the Flint Multitool.

### Clay Era
Appears after obtaining the Flint Multitool and guides the player through dirt/clay handling, the Baked Kiln, Sugar Cane processing, high-heat clay, and the Clay Grinder.

### Wood Era
Appears after obtaining the Clay Grinder and guides the player through glass/mortar infrastructure, Advanced Kiln casting, the Flint Axe, first worked wood, Woven Wood, construction branches, and the Wooden Grinder.

## Writing rule
Advancement descriptions are mini-guides: one direct sentence telling the player what action to attempt next. Exact percentages, durability totals, niche restrictions, and long mechanical explanations belong in tooltips/reference UI rather than the advancement tree.

## Recipe guide
The Project Flint recipe guide is available from the survival inventory's recipe-book button and from the Clay Grinder, Wooden Grinder, and Advanced Kiln GUIs.

Stations currently listed:
- Permanent Campfire
- Baked Kiln
- Mortar Kiln
- Clay Grinder
- Wooden Grinder
- Advanced Kiln

The left/right controls change station and Prev/Next page through that station's recipes.
