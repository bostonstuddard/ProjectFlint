# 0005 — Hardcore world rules and starter-biome spawn

## Status
Accepted in 0.1.0-alpha.4.

## Decision
Non-creative Project Flint play is treated as Hardcore, difficulty is forced and locked to Hard, and the initial Overworld spawn is relocated into a mild green starter biome when vanilla chooses something outside the starter set. Adventure mode is rejected; living players cannot opt into Spectator. Spectator remains available to Minecraft's post-death Hardcore flow.

The alpha.4 starter set is Plains, Sunflower Plains, Forest, Flower Forest, and Birch Forest.

## Why
The opening progression assumes access to forage, grass, trees, and loose stone. A desert, ocean edge, or extreme mountain start can turn the intended survival problem into seed roulette. The spawn restriction preserves biome exploration while making the first technological loop consistently testable.
