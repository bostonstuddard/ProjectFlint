# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.6 — Primitive Inventory Polish

## Intent
Apply only the four corrections from the alpha.5 playtest: finish hiding the personal-crafting UI, lighten locked-slot visuals, make failed stone-working consume its input correctly, and introduce the requested tiny primitive stack limits.

## Player-facing behavior
- The survival inventory no longer renders the vanilla `Crafting` foreground label above the removed 2x2 crafting region.
- Locked inventory slots use a lighter gray treatment while remaining clearly unavailable.
- Every completed stone-working attempt consumes one Loose Rock from the main hand. On success it becomes a Refined Rock; on failure that worked stone is lost. The offhand Loose Rock acts as the hammerstone and is not consumed.
- Loose Rock stacks to 1.
- Refined Rock stacks to 2.
- Twig stacks to 4.
- Plant Fiber stacks to 8.
- The creative-only placeable Loose Rock block item also stacks to 1 for consistency.

## Architecture introduced
No new subsystem is introduced. Inventory label suppression is moved to the actual `InventoryScreen.renderLabels` pass rather than adding another foreground cover. Primitive item carrying limits remain native item max-stack properties so every container/inventory path respects them automatically.

## Design reasoning
The crafting label is not part of the inventory background texture, which is why alpha.5's background mask could not remove it. Cancelling the survival inventory's label pass removes it at its source without covering dragged items. Stone working now treats the main-hand stone as the material being worked and the offhand stone as the reusable hammerstone; a completed failed attempt therefore destroys the worked stone instead of providing a free retry. Small stack limits reinforce the existing five-slot primitive carrying constraint without adding another custom inventory rule.

## Tuning snapshot
- Loose Rock max stack: 1.
- Refined Rock max stack: 2.
- Twig max stack: 4.
- Plant Fiber max stack: 8.
- Stone-working success chance remains 68%.
- Grass/bush timings and all mob/world rules are unchanged from alpha.5.
