# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.17 — Early-Fire Restoration, Vanilla Drops, and Water Pressure

## Intent

Alpha.17 is a focused repair pass on top of alpha.16. It restores the original Lightly Burnt Twig early-game role, makes Burnt Twig the later fire material it was intended to be, returns dropped-item behavior to Minecraft's vanilla item-entity path, increases Sugar Cane availability, clarifies Sweet Berry Bush harvesting, and adds water survival pressure through hostile-in-water Squids and increased swimming exhaustion.

No unrelated tool stats, multitool outputs, campfire duration, primitive inventory capacity, forage yields, or earlier accepted timings are retuned.

## Player-facing behavior

### Early fire is restored

- A successfully lit 12-Twig disposable Twig Pile still burns for 10 seconds.
- While lit, **Twig → Lightly Burnt Twig** again. This is the original early-game fire material used by the first crude-tool progression.
- **Burnt Twig is not an early Twig Pile output.** It is now first unlocked after the persistent campfire is available.
- The permanent campfire frame is constructed from **12 Campfire Stones + 12 Lightly Burnt Twigs**.
- Once lit, the permanent campfire performs **Twig → Burnt Twig** and **Branch → Burnt Branch**.
- The existing later conversion **1 Burnt Twig + Crude Stone Hammer → 2 Lightly Burnt Twigs** remains available.

This removes alpha.16's accidental circular progression where the permanent campfire required Burnt Twigs while Burnt Twigs were meant to belong to later fire progression.

### Sugar Cane availability

Sugar Cane receives an additional half-strength generation pass wherever vanilla already schedules the equivalent Sugar Cane feature. The target is approximately **1.5× vanilla generation attempts** without introducing Sugar Cane to unrelated biome families. This affects **newly generated chunks only**; existing explored terrain is not retroactively populated.

### Vanilla dropped items restored

Project Flint no longer intercepts/replaces `ItemEntity#playerTouch`. Q-dropped and world-spawned item entities use vanilla behavior for:

- appearing on the ground;
- initial velocity and gravity;
- pickup delay;
- merging;
- vanilla pickup transaction/sound/stat behavior.

The five-slot primitive carrying rule remains. Instead of modifying the world item, vanilla inventory insertion is limited to legal Project Flint destination slots.

### Sweet Berry Bushes

- Sweet Berry Bushes are now allowed through the primitive destroy-progress boundary and can be broken.
- Empty-hand right-click harvesting is blocked.
- Right-click harvesting with non-cutter items/tools is blocked, including higher stone stages that should not act as berry-harvesting tools.
- **Crude Stone Cutters** pass through to vanilla Sweet Berry Bush right-click harvesting, preserving normal renewable berry picking with the intended tool.

### Water pressure

Squids become territorial while the player is actually in water. A nearby Squid can acquire a survival player in water, swim toward that player, and attack at close range; Creative/Spectator players are ignored and the behavior disengages when there is no valid in-water target.

Active swimming movement now produces **3× Project Flint's previous swimming exhaustion**. Other exhaustion categories retain their existing tuning.

## Architecture

`InventoryMixin` replaces the old active `ItemEntityMixin` boundary. It constrains vanilla `Inventory#getFreeSlot` / `getSlotWithRemainingSpace` selection rather than cancelling item pickup. This keeps vanilla world-item semantics intact while preserving Project Flint carrying restrictions.

`PrimitiveExhaustionContext` now has an explicit swimming movement mode selected from `ServerPlayerMovementMixin`, avoiding a blanket hunger multiplier.

`SquidMixin` augments existing vanilla Squid AI server-side rather than replacing the entity registration. The aggro, chase speed, range, cooldown, and damage values remain centralized in `GameplayTuning`.

`ModWorldgen` adds half-frequency Project Flint placed features only in biome contexts that already contain the matching vanilla Sugar Cane placed feature. The configured plant feature itself remains vanilla.

Sweet Berry Bush left-click and right-click capabilities are kept separate: the block-breaking mixin allows normal destroy progress while `PrimitiveInteractionSystem` gates renewable right-click harvesting.

## Design reasoning

The Lightly Burnt/Burnt distinction is a progression-tier distinction, not a rename. Lightly Burnt Twig belongs to the disposable-fire opening and must exist before the first permanent-fire technology. Burnt Twig belongs after that milestone. Requiring Burnt Twig to build the thing that first unlocks Burnt Twig would be a softlock, so the frame correctly consumes the already-available Lightly Burnt form.

Dropped items are a vanilla system with many linked behaviors. Replacing the pickup method to enforce inventory capacity made unrelated drop behavior fragile. Moving the rule to destination-slot selection keeps the restriction at the smallest relevant boundary.

Sugar Cane is increased by adding half of each vanilla frequency rather than multiplying every vegetation pass or forcing cane into new biomes.

## Assets and licenses

No new visual/audio assets are added in alpha.17. The approved user-provided asset packs and alpha.16 provenance entries remain unchanged, so `docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md` and `docs/TEMPORARY_ASSETS.md` require no new entries for this repair pass.

## Advancement coverage

The fire branch is corrected so `charred_twig` represents the restored **Lightly Burnt Twig** early milestone. A later `burnt_twig` milestone branches from permanent campfire ignition, and the Burnt Twig → Lightly Burnt Twig hammer-processing milestone follows it. The Crude Multitool milestone now depends on the later Burnt Twig milestone, matching its actual material requirement.

## Validation

- Project Flint JSON/resource validation is updated for alpha.17.
- The validator checks the restored early-fire output, later Burnt Twig milestone, campfire component material, active mixin set, vanilla item-drop boundary, Sugar Cane supplemental placed features, Sweet Berry Bush interaction split, swimming exhaustion context, Squid water hostility, and documentation markers.
- The full Java source tree passes a Java 21 compile/type check against the cached Minecraft 1.21.11 and remapped Fabric API classes with compile-only stubs for artifact-environment dependencies. Those stubs are not shipped.
- Fabric Loom 1.14.10 remains unavailable from this environment's local plugin cache, so the normal Gradle/Fabric runtime build remains a local release gate rather than being claimed as completed here.

## Known limitations

- Increased Sugar Cane density is world-generation-only and cannot affect already-generated chunks without explicit retrogen, which alpha.17 does not add.
- Squid hostility is intentionally a narrow water-pressure behavior, not a generalized aquatic combat framework. Its first tuning values should be playtested before adding more aquatic predators.
- The five-slot restriction still intentionally changes where vanilla inventory insertion may place items; the world `ItemEntity` behavior itself is no longer replaced.

## Next release pressure

Playtest the opening from a fresh world through the first permanent campfire to confirm Lightly Burnt Twig is available at the original point, Burnt Twig cannot be acquired prematurely, and no circular material requirement remains. Also stress-test Q-dropping, dropping full/partial stacks, item merging, full five-slot pickup, Sweet Berry Bush interactions with empty hand/Refined Stone/Very Refined Stone/Cutters, swimming hunger, Squid disengagement on leaving water, and Sugar Cane density in newly generated terrain.
