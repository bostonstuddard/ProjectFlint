# Project Flint v0.31.58-b — Forge, Torch, Basin, Glass, Cups and Fertilizer

## Intent

Fix unintended automatic Forge fuel use, improve lighting and materials progression, rebalance Basin dust byproducts, add tier-specific early cup crafting and meaningful fertilizer.

## Player-facing behavior

- Forge does not automatically relight after burning out. A charcoal-fueled attempt needs a valid Hardwood or MBSSIG input and output room. To begin another batch, use a Firestarter manually.
- Fat-Burning Torch lasts 50 minutes (60,000 ticks) for every loaded Fat charge. It cannot ignite below Y=0; lit torches still obey rain extinguishing.
- Pure Mica Insulated Glass is a placeable dark blue-gray glass block, blocks light like vanilla Tinted Glass, and drops itself when broken.
- Raw Copper Slag chance 12.5%; Rare Earth from Clay, Gravel and Sand 4% each; Rare Earth from Slag 1.8%. All byproduct chances are separate, so all may succeed simultaneously.
- Flint Plant Cutters sticks left; Flint top-middle/middle-right/bottom-middle.
- Bone Knife + 1 Wooden Sheet → 3 Wooden Cups in 2×2, 4 in Hardwood Table; loses durability. Two Cups + Clay Milk Bucket → 5 Raw Milk Cups in 2×2, 6 at Hardwood Table; empty Clay Bucket returned.
- Hardwood Table: 8 Flax Seeds around Clay Water Bucket → 6 Wheat Seeds. 8 Fertilizer around Potassium Dust → 12 Potassium-Rich Fertilizer. Fertilizer acts like Bone Meal for crops/other supported plants, not tree saplings or tree fungi.

## Architecture

Forge `ignite()` is manual-only and input-gated for charcoal. Glass uses a `TintedGlassBlock` and block item, translucent chunk layer, new blockstate/model/loot. `BasinMaterialProcessingSystem` uses individual drop checks. The Clay Milk Bucket now declares `CLAY_BUCKET` as crafting remainder. New Potassium-Rich Fertilizer subclasses `BoneMealItem`.

## Design reasoning and tuning

Fuel should never be burnt unintentionally. Torch changes preserve Fat consumption and make light conservation meaningful underground. Each optional Basin byproduct rolls independently. Personal and Hardwood recipe tiers have distinct productivity. Fertilizer intentionally excludes instant tree growth. Exact Basin odds are in `docs/BASIN_MATERIAL_YIELDS.md`.
