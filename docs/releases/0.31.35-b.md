# Project Flint v0.31.35-b — Fireclay Forge and Guide Corrections

## Structure (Creative-only controller)
The controller replaces the outward-facing front-centre fireclay brick at Y1. Coordinates relative to the centre of the 3x3 solid base:

- Y0: all nine cells are Fireclay Brick Blocks.
- Y1: brick ring with hollow/replaceable dry centre; controller at outward-facing front-centre.
- Y2: fireclay stairs in all four corners, fireclay bricks in the four cardinal positions; empty centre.
- Y3–Y4: four cardinal fireclay brick blocks (plus/cross outline); corners and centre open.
- Y5: four **single** Fireclay Brick Slabs at the cardinals; centre/corners open.
- Y6: clear smoke exit directly above the centre.

Air/replaceable non-fluid cells are accepted. The validator checks each ordinary casing block by type without assigning ownership, so wall sharing between otherwise valid forge structures is allowed. Stairs may be rotated freely; slab must not be double. The controller continues to show formed/unformed and emits chimney smoke, crackle and light while burning.

## Fuel rather than a one-off charcoal recipe
- Visible inventory: future recipe-material input (currently unused); separate fuel slot; existing firestarter ignition slot; separate charcoal byproduct output slot.
- 1 overworld log/wood (stripped or normal, Minecraft `logs_that_burn`) burns for 1200 ticks / 60 seconds, generating 1 charcoal every 300 ticks / 15 seconds (four byproducts total).
- 1 vanilla Charcoal burns for 6000 ticks / 5 minutes; no byproducts.
- Every initial or automatic fuel ignition costs one Firestarter durability. The system checks fuel, starter, formation and output capacity before consuming them.
- The fire pauses instead of voiding the next charcoal when the output slot is full; an incomplete structure also pauses the current fuel. Inventory, fuel type, remaining burn time and production counter are persisted.
- Old v0.31.34-b Forge inventories migrate to the new layout. Prepaid wood is retained as hidden legacy fuel, automatically moved to the normal fuel slot when it becomes vacant and dropped if the controller is dismantled.
- Future material/metal recipes are **not** invented by this patch. The standalone PF-EMI mod remains the sole EMI owner.

## Interface and collision
- Recipe Guide button added to Forge GUI, with a global Forge page explaining current charcoal fuel/byproduct operation and firestarter use. No wood-specific hint remains on the future recipe-input slot.
- The dynamically populated guide now clips overwide recipe output names to their row, scrolls horizontally with a pause at both ends, and exposes a tooltip on the name itself.
- Basin and Drying Rack are 8/16 block tall; Millstone is 10/16. Their selection/collision volumes use inset low-profile model bounds.
- New `docs/RECIPE_GUIDE_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` plus validator checks make guide entry, gameplay recipe registry routing, and workstation button review mandatory for future releases.

## Validation and limits
Run `python tools/validate_release.py` and `build.bat` on your complete v0.31.34-b source after applying this patch. A standalone local compile and in-world smoke/shape checks are still required before reporting a successful complete Gradle build. No survival crafting recipe is registered for the Forge or three prototype stations.

## Intent
Correct user-reported forge shape and heat/fuel semantics without creating unrequested metallurgy, and repair guide readability and model-sized prototype hitboxes.

## Player-facing behavior
The Forge reports formed status only for the full six-layer outline. Its fuel slot is independent from the future recipe input. Wood and charcoal provide different heat durations; wood has a non-wasting charcoal byproduct. Guide names now scroll instead of overflowing.

## Architecture
The multiblock remains world-state validation without exclusive wall ownership. Forge fuel state is persisted by its BlockEntity and synchronized to its menu. Recipes remain authoritative in ProjectFlint gameplay systems while PF-EMI provides external recipe discovery; no EMI API integration was added.

## Design reasoning
Keeping recipe inputs and fuels separate allows later metal processing without having to replace the heat system. Preventing burn advance when a byproduct output is blocked protects fuel and results. One shared source for Guide station wiring plus a release checklist reduces omissions.

## Tuning snapshot
Wood fuel: 1200 ticks, charcoal every 300 ticks. Charcoal fuel: 6000 ticks, no output. One Firestarter durability per unit. Forge shape Y0 solid, Y1 ring/controller, Y2 stair corners, Y3–4 cardinals, Y5 single slab caps, Y6 clear smoke exit.

## Assets and licenses
No additional externally sourced visual assets. Previously supplied fireclay/controller and workstation textures are unchanged; this patch edits geometry/collision/logic and existing UI only.

## Known limitations
Forge is Creative-only and currently has only heat and wood-fuel charcoal byproduct, not metallurgy or additional recipes. Full local Gradle Java compile and in-game formation/fuel/save/load verification are still needed; the static validator does not substitute for playtesting.

## Next release pressure
Playtest all four orientations, corner-stair and slab orientations, adjacent shared-wall structures, full output pause/resume, auto-refuel durability, save migration from v0.31.34-b, and the guide marquee in scaled GUIs.
