# Project Flint v0.31.0-b

## Intent
This beta expands the Wood/Water transition while repairing several progression blockers and presentation regressions found during playtesting. The release keeps the existing one-page, quest-heavy advancement guidance and adds the new storage, woodworking, brushing, water-vessel, armor, and workstation steps without reopening unrelated balance decisions.

## Player-facing behavior
- Sugar Cane can be planted on Loose Dirt. Ordinary blocks can once again replace water where vanilla placement permits it.
- Recovered Lumber Processors place their three-cell footprint horizontally across the player. While formed, linked Water Pot/Crushed Stone parts cannot be mined; the processor/controller remains the deliberate dismantle point.
- Leaves take about 2.5 s with Crude Multitool, 2.0 s with Flint Multitool, and 1.5 s with Flint Plant Cutters/Advanced Flint Multitool. Flint Plant Cutters use vanilla leaf loot so saplings and normal leaf drops are obtainable.
- Both grinders gain Crude Clay Block -> 4 Fired Clay and Branch -> 3 Twig. The Advanced Kiln gains Fired Clay Block Mold + 8 Burnt Branches + 8 Unfired Clay -> 3 Crude Clay Blocks in 20 s, without consuming the mold.
- Sanding Station is quarter-block tall; 3 Sand Flakes -> 1 Sand Ball and Advanced Kiln Glass now costs 3 Glass Shards. Wooden Grinder adds the requested Sanding Paper, Wood Firestarter, Bone Hoe, Leather Boots/Helmet, Bookshelf, and full Bone Brush -> Brush recipes.
- Water Pots are non-directional, easy to break by hand, preserve 0/0.5/1 bucket contents when moved, and a full placed pot can wash Dirt Pile -> Mud Pile or Reed Roll -> Sugarcane Roll without spending water.
- Wooden Chest is made by using 16 Wooden Boards on a Woven Chest. It has 9 slots alone and forms an 18-slot, two-row double chest using vanilla chest connection/opening behavior.
- Wooden Beam is now a block but cannot be placed in Survival yet. Acacia, Birch, Cherry, Crimson, Dark Oak, Jungle, Mangrove, Pale Oak, Spruce, and Warped Wooden Beam variants are Creative-only. Sawmill is also Creative-only for now.
- Bookshelf is made from 4 Wooden Beams + 4 Wooden Boards. Its workstation makes a vanilla Book from 8 Sugarcane Rolls + 2 Leather. Inserting four Books fills the shelf and records the filled state for future recipe unlocks; the filled/unfilled state survives moving the block.
- The Project Flint Brush has 512 durability, brushes Sand/Red Sand into Clay one second faster than Bone Brush, gives 2 Sand Flakes, gives 1 Flint + 2 Flint Chips from Gravel, and yields one extra Wool when used on sheep.
- Bone armor uses Filed Bone at vanilla armor material counts in either grinder, has Leather-equivalent protection, and 128 durability per piece. Bone Hoe has 24 durability, only tills Grass Blocks touching water, and has a 10% chance to yield currently-inert Flax Seeds.
- Default newly placed Sleeping Bags are green.

## Architecture
`WaterPotItem` stores vessel fill level in custom item data and restores it into `WaterPotBlockEntity` on placement. The Water Pot block no longer has horizontal facing state. `LumberProcessorSystem` remains the authority for formed-machine linkage and now also supplies the sideways recovered-placement orientation.

`WoodenChestBlock` subclasses vanilla `ChestBlock`; a 9-slot `WoodenChestBlockEntity`, one/two-row menu pair, chest renderer/material context, and chest atlas entries preserve vanilla single/double chest mechanics while using Project Flint textures. `BookshelfBlockEntity` stores two crafting inputs plus a persistent 0-4 inserted-book count.

The special 512-durability vanilla Brush is identified by its `MAX_DAMAGE` component rather than replacing `minecraft:brush`. `BrushItemMixin` only redirects the brushing timing/output path for that Project Flint-authored stack; vanilla brushes remain vanilla. New beam/Sawmill `CreativeOnlyBlockItem` instances deny Survival placement while retaining Creative development access.

## Design reasoning
The leaf-speed reduction restores gathering friction without removing established tool progression. Plant Cutters intentionally fall back to vanilla leaf loot because saplings are now a required renewable-tree entry point. Water Pot state is stored on the item so moving water storage is convenient rather than punitive, while formed Lumber Processor parts remain protected from accidental partial teardown. The Bookshelf is introduced before its future recipe catalog so the physical knowledge-progression gate can exist without inventing recipes early.

## Tuning snapshot
- Leaf break times: Crude Multitool ~2.5 s; Flint Multitool ~2.0 s; Flint Plant Cutters / Advanced Flint Multitool ~1.5 s.
- Advanced Kiln Crude Clay Block process: 20 s; 8 Burnt Branches; 8 Unfired Clay; Fired Clay Block Mold retained; output 3.
- Woven Chest Frame: 3 Leather + 6 Woven Wood.
- Sanding Paper: 3 Sugarcane Rolls + 1 Plant Tool Binding -> 2.
- Project Flint Brush: 512 durability; 2.5 s brushing; 2 Sand Flakes; Gravel -> 1 Flint + 2 Flint Chips; sheep Wool 4-5.
- Wood Firestarter: 144 uses (1.5x the 96-use Bone Firestarter).
- Bone armor: Leather-equivalent attributes, 128 durability each. Bone Hoe: 24 uses, 10% Flax Seed chance.
- Wooden Chest: 9 slots single / 18 slots double. Bookshelf fill requirement: 4 Books.

## Assets and licenses
All active PNGs imported from `new update stuff(4).zip` are direct owner-supplied Project Flint assets copied byte-for-byte. Exact target/source SHA-256 mappings are appended to `docs/TEXTURE_PROVENANCE.md`. The supplied Bound Wooden Processor sprite is intentionally not activated because the explicit design instruction for this release is to render that item from the Lumber Processor top texture. Beam strip sheets are also not used because individual beam textures were supplied. The Sawmill example JSON is used only as a geometry reference with Project Flint namespace wiring; its PNGs remain unmodified.

## Advancement coverage
The single `ProjectFlint` advancement page remains authoritative. v0.31.0-b adds detailed milestones for Wooden Chest, Bookshelf, crafting a Book there, filling the Bookshelf, Wood Firestarter, Bone Hoe, Flax Seeds, all four Bone armor pieces, and the upgraded 512-durability Brush. Existing Woven Chest Frame, Sanding Paper, Advanced Kiln, Wooden Beam, and Water Pot guidance is updated for the new counts/behavior.

## Known limitations
The Sawmill and non-Oak beam variants are deliberately Creative-only placeholders for future progression. Wooden Beam itself may be produced in Survival but cannot yet be placed there. The Bookshelf has no filled-shelf recipes yet; Book is its only current craft. Flax Seeds are obtainable but intentionally have no planting/use behavior yet.

The artifact environment cannot complete the normal Gradle build because Fabric Loom `1.14.10` is not available to the offline plugin resolver. Release validation therefore covers source/resource invariants and JSON/provenance integrity; a local `build.bat` compile remains the final runtime gate.
