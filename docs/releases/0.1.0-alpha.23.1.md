# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.23.1

## Advanced tool combat attributes
- Flint Axe: 256 durability, 5.75 final attack damage, 0.60 final attack speed. Uses main-hand `ADD_VALUE` modifiers of +4.75 attack damage and -3.40 attack speed.
- Flint Plant Cutters: 256 durability, 4.25 final attack damage, 1.40 final attack speed. Uses main-hand `ADD_VALUE` modifiers of +3.25 attack damage and -2.60 attack speed.
- Advanced Flint Multitool: 512 durability, 4.85 final attack damage, 1.25 final attack speed. Uses main-hand `ADD_VALUE` modifiers of +3.85 attack damage and -2.75 attack speed.

The existing `minecraft:tool` component, durability, harvesting rules, and special utility behavior remain unchanged.
