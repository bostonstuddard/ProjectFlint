# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.9 — Knapping & Twig Pile Polish

## Intent
Fix the two issues reported after the first-fire playtest without retuning unrelated accepted systems: successful stone working appearing to produce no Refined Rock, and the visually poor stacked-Twig ground model.

## Player-facing behavior
- A successful completed stone-working attempt now delivers the Refined Rock through Project Flint's primitive five-slot inventory path instead of vanilla unrestricted inventory insertion.
- The 68% success chance, eight strikes per attempt, worked-rock consumption, and offhand hammerstone behavior are unchanged.
- Twig Piles are visually flatter and read as individual overlapping twigs on the ground instead of a staircase/tower of rectangular wooden bars.
- The pile still visibly gains one twig per placed Twig from 1 through 12.
- Ignition, 12-Twig capacity, 50% fire-start chance, 10-second burn duration, and Lightly Burnt Twig behavior are unchanged.

## Architecture introduced
No new gameplay subsystem is introduced. `StoneWorkingSystem` now uses the existing `PrimitiveInteractionSystem.giveOrDrop` insertion path for successful Refined Rock output, keeping progression rewards inside the same five-slot carrying rules as other primitive resources.

`TwigPileBlock` remains state-driven with `count` and `lit`. Its 12 block models were rebuilt as thin cutout sprite cards using a dedicated temporary block-atlas Twig texture. `ProjectFlintClient` explicitly assigns the Twig Pile to the CUTOUT layer.

## Design reasoning
The knapping bug was treated as an output-delivery problem rather than a reason to alter the accepted 68% success rate. Vanilla `Inventory#add` can search inventory space Project Flint intentionally locks; routing the result through the primitive inventory helper makes successful output deterministic with respect to the five allowed slots and drops it visibly when those slots cannot accept it.

The old Twig Pile model used one chunky cuboid per Twig and increased height almost every placement. At 12 Twigs it looked like a wooden staircase rather than a small pile of kindling. The new presentation keeps all 12 count states while spreading overlapping twig silhouettes across a low footprint.

## Tuning snapshot
- Stone-working strikes per attempt: unchanged at 8.
- Stone-working success chance: unchanged at 68%.
- Completed attempt worked-rock cost: unchanged at 1.
- Offhand hammerstone cost: unchanged at 0.
- Twig Pile capacity: unchanged at 12.
- Ignition chance: unchanged at 50%.
- Lit duration: unchanged at 10 seconds.
- Grass strip time: unchanged (`1 / 156` progress/tick).
- Bush harvest time: unchanged (`1 / 60` progress/tick).

## Assets and licensing
No third-party art is introduced. The new Twig Pile uses Project Flint's existing temporary Twig sprite copied into a dedicated block texture (`textures/block/twig_pile_twig.png`) solely so the block model can render it reliably through the cutout block atlas. The geometry and texture remain explicitly temporary and are tracked in `docs/TEMPORARY_ASSETS.md`.
