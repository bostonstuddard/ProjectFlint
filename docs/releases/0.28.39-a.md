# Project Flint v0.28.39-a — Opening Pacing & Advancement Restoration

## Intent
Make the first minutes less dependent on bad RNG and restore the advancement screen as Project Flint's detailed, quest-like progression manual.

## Player-facing behavior
- Hand-scraping Grass Blocks is faster and has a 58% chance to produce Plant Fiber.
- Regular Rock knapping keeps its 68% base chance, but after three consecutive failures the next attempt is guaranteed.
- Twig Pile ignition chance rises from 50% to 58%.
- Crude Stone Cutters receive at most 1 Plant Fiber from a full Leaf Litter patch; better tools retain their progression bonuses.
- True-black night arrives a little later during dusk, Terrified waits 10 continuous seconds before appearing, and the existing 45-second damage grace remains intact.
- The Tool/Clay/Wood advancement tabs are replaced by one detailed ProjectFlint progression menu containing 138 guided milestones.

## Architecture
`GameplayTuning` owns the adjusted opening probabilities/speed. `StoneWorkingSystem` owns the per-player regular-Rock failure streak. `PrimitiveEnvironmentEffectSystem` separates effect-entry grace from damage grace. The advancement data returns to a single `projectflint:root` tree, while the custom workstation recipe guide remains independent.

## Design reasoning
Early Project Flint should feel harsh because of preparation and survival decisions, not because a player can repeatedly lose to the same opening RNG gate. Detailed advancements are intentionally allowed to be more verbose than conventional Minecraft achievements because they act as the in-game tutorial/quest book.

## Tuning snapshot
- Grass scrape: 140 ticks (~7.0 s)
- Plant Fiber chance: 58%
- Rock knapping: 68% base; guaranteed next attempt after 3 failures
- Twig Pile ignition: 58%
- Terrified effect entry grace: 10 s
- Terrified damage grace: 45 s from entering qualifying darkness
- Crude Stone Cutters Leaf Litter maximum: 1 Plant Fiber

## Assets and licenses
No texture pixels or external assets are added or modified in this release.

## Advancement coverage
The detailed pre-era-split progression is restored, including tool upgrades, material intermediates, fire, clay, molds, storage, construction, and workstation milestones. The later Sanding Station, transported-water, and Lumber Processor milestones are retained and connected into the same tree.

## Known limitations
The regular-Rock pity streak is runtime player state rather than persistent save data; restarting the server/client clears an incomplete failure streak. This does not affect already-created items or advancement progress.

## Validation
The release validator checks the single advancement root, parent integrity, milestone count, updated tuning constants, knapping pity logic, darkness grace, and removal of the era-root folders.

## Next release pressure
Playtest the full opening again before increasing scarcity elsewhere. The next priority should be making sure the restored advancement path stays accurate as Water Age content is added.
