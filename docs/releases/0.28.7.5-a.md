# Project Flint v0.28.7.5-a — Knapping Safety, Night Grace, and Crude Plant Torch

## Intent
Fix the remaining whole-stack loss in primitive knapping, slow the kiln face animation, keep midnight oppressive without making the immediate surroundings completely invisible, extend the darkness survival window, and add a deliberately dangerous short-lived primitive light source.

## Player-facing behavior
- Finishing a Rock-on-Rock knapping attempt consumes exactly one Rock from the main hand whether the attempt succeeds or fails. A success grants one Refined Rock; the rest of the held Rock stack remains intact.
- The active Advanced/Mortar Kiln front animation now runs at half its previous speed (4 ticks per frame instead of 2).
- Full night remains black beyond the short fog envelope, but nearby terrain retains enough moonlight to be barely readable inside roughly 6 blocks.
- Darkness damage does not begin until the player has remained in qualifying darkness for 45 seconds.
- A Lightly Burnt Twig used on a lit Twig Pile or lit permanent Campfire becomes one Crude Plant Torch.
- Crude Plant Torches stack to 1 and burn for 5 minutes total across carried and placed states.
- A carried torch deals 0.5 heart every 2.5 seconds, continues consuming its burn timer, and counts as effective light level 6 at the carrier for the darkness/Terrified survival check.
- A carried torch immediately burns out in water, exposed rain, or when the player no longer has direct sky access.
- A placed torch emits light level 6, cannot be recovered, disappears when its remaining timer expires, and also extinguishes in rain/water or without direct sky access.
- Exposed placed torches can ignite adjacent combustible wood/vegetation.

## Architecture
- `RockItem` now uses the same explicit decrement/output transaction style as Project Flint's other stack-safe processing instead of `InteractionResult.heldItemTransformedTo`.
- `CrudePlantTorchItem` uses native item damage as its 6000-tick burn timer, allowing the remaining lifetime to travel with the ItemStack without a parallel player-data store.
- `CrudePlantTorchBlockEntity` owns the placed remaining lifetime and environmental/fire-spread checks. Placement copies the remaining item lifetime into the block entity before the BlockItem is consumed.
- `CrudePlantTorchBlock` is an unrecoverable, block-entity-backed light source with light level 6.

## Design reasoning
The knapping failure was a transaction-semantics bug rather than a stack-size tuning issue: a whole-hand transformation is correct only when the input stack must be replaced wholesale. Primitive knapping consumes one workpiece, so an explicit one-item decrement is the correct invariant even if future Rock stack sizes change again.

The night presentation now separates two goals: a black distance cutoff and a very small amount of nearby moonlit readability. The Crude Plant Torch is intentionally poor permanent infrastructure: it is short-lived, hurts while carried, requires open sky, fails in water/rain, is unrecoverable once placed, and threatens nearby combustible blocks.

## Tuning snapshot
- Rock knapping consumption: 1 main-hand Rock per completed attempt.
- Active kiln front animation: 4 ticks/frame.
- Full-night fog: starts near 6 blocks, completes near 8 blocks, black fog color.
- Full-night sky-light floor: 0.06 shader factor.
- Darkness damage grace: 45 seconds.
- Crude Plant Torch stack size: 1.
- Crude Plant Torch lifetime: 6000 ticks / 5 minutes.
- Carried burn damage: 1.0 health (0.5 heart) every 50 ticks / 2.5 seconds.
- Placed light level: 6.

## Assets and licenses
No new Project Flint texture pixels were generated or edited. The Crude Plant Torch item/block models reference Mojang's existing vanilla torch artwork directly. Existing owner-provided assets and Bear Minimum attribution remain unchanged.

## Advancement coverage
No new advancement was added for the Crude Plant Torch in this hotfix; this release establishes the mechanic first without inventing a new progression gate beyond the requested lit-fire interaction.

## Known limitations
- Carried torches count as effective light level 6 for the server-side darkness/Terrified check, but do not inject a moving block-light source into chunk lighting. The placed form supplies actual block light level 6.
- Full-night readability is shader/fog based and can vary slightly with display calibration, but the configured fog envelope remains 6–8 blocks.
- Full Gradle assembly still depends on resolving Fabric Loom 1.14.10 when it is not already cached.

## Validation
- JSON resources are parsed by `tools/validate_release.py`.
- The validator rejects any remaining Rock knapping `heldItemTransformedTo(...)` path and checks one-item consumption.
- It verifies the 4-tick active front animation, 0.06 night floor, 45-second darkness grace, Crude Plant Torch 6000-tick/50-tick tuning, environmental extinguishing, light level 6, unrecoverable block configuration, lit-campfire conversion hooks, and build/cleanup versioning.
- Full Gradle assembly was attempted offline; the environment cannot resolve the uncached Fabric Loom 1.14.10 plugin artifact, so no successful full Gradle build is claimed.

## Next release pressure
Playtest whether the 0.06 moonlight floor is readable enough inside the 6-block area without undermining the intended black night, and whether the Crude Plant Torch's 5-minute lifetime / 2.5-second carried burn interval makes it useful only as an emergency light rather than permanent infrastructure.
