# Project Flint 0.25.0

## Animal materials

- Chicken: 1 Animal Bones.
- Pig: 1-2 Animal Bones.
- Sheep: 1-3 Animal Bones.
- Cow: 2-3 Animal Bones.
- Squid: 1-3 Animal Bones.
- Sheep now replace their vanilla death-wool drop with 1-2 Project Flint Wool.

## Tool and slab corrections

- Flint Axe explicitly mines/retrieves Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Bamboo, Bamboo Mosaic, Crimson, and Warped slabs.
- Flint Axe also retrieves the new Sanded/Grinded/Woven Wood blocks and slabs.
- Mortar Brick Slabs and Crude Clay Slabs require Flint Multitool tier or better.

## Processed wood chain

1. Right-click any placed vanilla plank slab with Sanding Paper. The paper is consumed and the slab becomes the generic Sanded Wood Slab.
2. Clay Grinder: 2 Sanded Wood Slabs -> 1 Grinded Wood Slab.
3. Right-click a placed Grinded Wood Slab with a Woven Fan. The fan is consumed and the slab becomes a Woven Wood Slab.
4. Clay Grinder: 2 Woven Wood Slabs -> 1 Woven Wood.

Top/bottom/double slab state is preserved by the two world-processing steps. A double slab costs two processing items.

## Reversible slab processing

The Clay Grinder now provides reverse routes for Loose Dirt Slabs, Mortar Brick Slabs, Crude Clay Slabs, Grinded/Sanded Wood, Woven Wood, and all existing stripped-log -> vanilla wood-slab recipes.

## Assets

Sanded Wood, Grinded Wood, and Woven Wood use exact unmodified textures from the approved Project Flint asset master.
