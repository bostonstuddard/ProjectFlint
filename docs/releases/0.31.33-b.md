# Project Flint v0.31.33-b — Oven Baking & Workstation Prototypes

## Intent
Extend the researched Primitive Oven baking path, adjust prepared-food economics, and register three future workstation shells without gameplay recipes. PF-EMI remains external and the sole EMI presentation owner.

## Player-facing behavior
- Sushi Rolls: 2 Raw Calamari + 2 Flour + 1 Sugar -> 5 Sushi Rolls (requires Sushi parchment).
- Fat parchment: any 3 raw meat -> 5 Fat. Tooltip says only “any 3 raw meat.” The existing direct meat/water fat route is unchanged.
- Plain Bread is now baked from 2 Dough + 1 Sugar -> 1 vanilla Bread, superseding the old 3 Flour + 2 Raw Milk -> 2 Bread Oven recipe. The later dough-specific instruction takes precedence over the earlier request to adjust the old recipe to 2:3.
- 2 Flour + 1 Raw Milk -> 1 Dough, and 1 Dough + 1 Sugar -> 1 Sweet Dough (both researched Oven operations). All ten new bread products use one of these doughs, consume listed ingredients, and have individual Bookshelf parchments.
- New researched Oven efficiency option: 1 Egg -> 3 Scrambled Eggs after researching the parchment with 1 Bacon and Eggs. The direct primitive kiln/campfire 1 Egg -> 2 Scrambled Eggs path remains intact.
- Existing vanilla raw/cooked meat and basic crops get modest nutrition/saturation adjustments; ProjectFlint's researched prepared foods retain their values.
- Brick Furnace is now shown in the in-game recipe guide, including Leather -> Tanned Leather and its existing kiln recipe pool.
- Basin, Drying Rack, and Millstone are registered as Creative-accessible nonfunctional block/item prototypes, with no crafting recipes or inventories. The separate supplied Chopping Block asset is reserved; the existing functional Lumber Processor chopping block is unchanged.

## Architecture
PrimitiveStoveRecipeRegistry remains the gameplay authority; BookshelfRecipeRegistry unlocks bound parchments; the recipe guide reads those registries dynamically. No ProjectFlint EMI plugin or compile dependency is added. The placeholder workstations are plain blocks; their future behavior awaits a design release.

## Design reasoning
Parchment requirements allow controlled multi-input meals. The high-yield sushi recipe makes the Raw Calamari conversion worthwhile. Dough acts as the ingredient progression gate instead of allowing instant flour-to-bread cooking.

## Tuning snapshot
- Sushi: 2 Calamari + 2 Flour + Sugar -> 5.
- Fat research: 3 assorted Raw Meat -> 5 Fat.
- Dough: 2 Flour + 1 Raw Milk -> 1; Sweet Dough: 1 Dough + 1 Sugar -> 1.
- Plain Bread: 2 Dough + 1 Sugar -> 1.
- Rustic Bread: 1 Dough -> 1; Sweet Bread: 1 Sweet Dough -> 1.
- Scrambled Egg (Primitive Oven, parchment): 1 Egg -> 3; unlock requires 1 Bacon and Eggs at a full Bookshelf.
- Flatbread: 2 Dough + 1 Fat -> 2; French Bread: 3 Dough + 1 Raw Milk -> 2.
- Meat Bread: 2 Dough + 2 cooked meat + 1 Fat -> 2; Cheese Bread: 2 Dough + 1 Cheese -> 2.
- Egg Bread: 2 Dough + 2 Scrambled Egg -> 2; Breakfast Bread: 2 Sweet Dough + 2 Scrambled Egg + 1 Butter -> 2.
- Apple Bread: 2 Sweet Dough + 2 Apples -> 2; Pumpkin Bread: 2 Sweet Dough + 4 Pumpkin Slices -> 2.

## Assets and licenses
The twelve directly supplied dough/bread textures are used byte-for-byte. The stitched Basin, Drying Rack and Millstone textures from the supplied `new workstations.zip` are also byte-identical. The original Drying Rack geometry was adapted only by replacing its asset namespace. The archive's Chopping Block was not introduced as a fourth new workstation; ProjectFlint already has a gameplay chopping block.

## Advancement coverage
Legacy advancements remain intentionally disabled. Flint Quests integration and event hooks are unchanged. The Bookshelf research recipes and built-in recipe guide expose all new researched foods.

## Known limitations
Basin/Drying Rack/Millstone are art and registration prototypes only. Neither their recipes nor processing behavior has been specified. Full Gradle compilation and runtime resource/model checks require a local Loom-enabled build; release validator checks are source-level.

## Validation
Source and asset validator, release-diff audit, and patch-over-prior release simulation are required. Do not describe a full Gradle build as passed unless it actually compiles.

## Next release pressure
Define workstation inventory, recipes and mechanical behavior and coordinate PF-EMI's separately maintained presentation pages.
