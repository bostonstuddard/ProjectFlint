# Project Flint v0.31.40-b

## Intent
Correct wooden pickaxe access to masonry, slow Stone mining and forage bush block recovery.

## Player-facing behavior
- The Wooden Pickaxe now mines Brick, Brick Shingles, Fireclay Bricks (block/slab/stairs), Mortar Bricks and Slabs, Wattled Daub brick construction, limestone bricks, and Loose Mud Bricks. Double slabs return two pieces. Brick machines/Forge Controller retain their inventory-drop paths and also accept the Wooden Pickaxe.
- Stone and Crushed Stone take approximately 13.5 seconds with a Wooden Pickaxe (before Tool Belt bonus). Stone yields Cobblestone and Crushed Stone yields Very Refined Stone as before.
- Active and legacy forage bushes can be removed without specialized tools; this drops one vanilla Stick. Refining/Cutters still produce their previous Twig outputs.

## Architecture
Brick mining capability is centralized in `ToolCapabilities.canMineBrick`, while the simple masonry target list is shared in `WoodenToolTargets.brickMasonry`. Permission, mining-progress and completed-harvest handlers agree. Existing registered legacy bushes are included in the forage tag without restoring their world generation.

## Design reasoning
Mining a regular Stone block must not bypass the Wooden Pickaxe timing through the Utility Vest's provisional player-placement preview. The Wooden Pickaxe path is evaluated before that preview. Custom server recovery preserves slab counts and does not bypass Brick Furnace/Forge inventory handling.

## Tuning snapshot
- Wooden Pickaxe on Stone and Crushed Stone: 1/270 mining progress per tick (13.5 seconds at 20 TPS), modified by Tool Belt ×1.10 if worn.
- Wooden Pickaxe on simple masonry: 1/120 mining progress per tick (6 seconds), modified by Tool Belt ×1.10 if worn.
- Unassisted forage bush: 1/40 per tick (2 seconds), one vanilla Stick. Existing dedicated cutters/rock timing and Twig quantities preserved.
