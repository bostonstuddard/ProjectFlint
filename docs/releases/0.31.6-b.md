# Project Flint v0.31.6-b

## Intent
Repair the integration faults exposed by playtesting the v0.31.5-b Limestone, Wooden Cabinet, and Primitive Stove systems without changing their intended progression.

## Player-facing behavior
Wooden Cabinets now render their oak cabinet body beneath the stone countertop, reject blank Recipe Parchment, and can be dismantled with the Flint Axe. All Limestone and Checkered Tile variants can be mined with the Advanced Flint Multitool and return their intended block/drop form. The Primitive Stove accepts stripped logs/wood/stems/hyphae as its four-unit ignition fuel, directly cooks ordinary food recipes again, can be mined with the Advanced Flint Multitool, and runs at the intended 1.30x baseline processing speed. All v0.31.5-b block items now have readable names.

## Architecture
The Limestone family remains handled by the custom destroy-progress and ServerPlayerGameMode drop paths; the missing survival pre-break authorization is now aligned with those systems. Stove speed is centralized in GameplayTuning. Cabinet storage validates RecipeParchmentItem metadata at both container and menu insertion boundaries.

## Design reasoning
This patch treats 1.0x as the shared baked-kiln baseline, with Primitive Campfire at 0.90x, Mortar Kiln at 1.25x, and Primitive Stove at 1.30x. The Stove therefore receives a 30% baseline boost rather than compounding a second 30% bonus on top of Mortar.

## Tuning snapshot
- Primitive Campfire: 0.90x baseline.
- Baked Kiln: 1.00x baseline.
- Mortar Kiln: 1.25x baseline.
- Primitive Stove: 1.30x baseline.
- Primitive Stove burn duration: 30 minutes per four stripped-wood fuel units.

## Assets and licenses
No new texture pixels were introduced. The existing owner-supplied cabinet/stone-counter assets are retained; this release only corrects their render-layer hookup.

## Advancement coverage
No advancement topology changes are required; this is a behavior/recovery hotfix for already documented v0.31.5-b progression.

## Known limitations
Recipe Parchment recipe-binding interaction is still intentionally deferred until the Bookshelf research/unlock interaction is specified. Existing blank parchment already present in old test cabinets is not automatically deleted.

## Validation
Release validation checks resource JSON, item names, cabinet render/storage guards, Stove fuel/direct-food/speed rules, and mining authorization for Limestone, Cabinet, and Stove.

## Next release pressure
Define Bookshelf recipe selection/binding for blank Recipe Parchment and begin assigning recipe metadata to unlockable Stove recipes.
