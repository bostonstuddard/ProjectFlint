# Project Flint v0.28.7.8-a

## Intent
Tighten early food progression, finish the first pumpkin/melon tool interactions, and remove an unintended carried-torch restriction without changing placed-torch survival rules.

## Player-facing behavior
- A lit Crude Plant Torch may now remain in the player's inventory under cover. It still burns down, hurts the carrier on its existing interval, and is destroyed by water/direct rain.
- Once placed, the Crude Plant Torch still requires direct sky access.
- Flint Axe converts Pumpkin -> 1 Pumpkin Seed and Melon -> 1 Melon Seed.
- Flint Plant Cutters or the Advanced Flint Multitool convert Pumpkin -> 1 Pumpkin Slice and Melon -> 1 Melon Slice.
- Pumpkin Slice and Melon Slice each restore 0.5 hunger and 0.5 saturation.
- Raw meats/fish are substantially weaker foods; cooked chicken, mutton, rabbit, and horse meat are also rebalanced as requested.

## Architecture
Gourd harvesting stays inside the existing deny-by-default primitive block-break path: `BlockStateBaseMixin` owns progress, `PrimitiveInteractionSystem` owns permission, and `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin` owns final output/durability. The torch distinction remains clean: carried behavior belongs to `CrudePlantTorchItem`, while sky-access checks stay in `CrudePlantTorchBlockEntity` for placed torches.

## Design reasoning
A carried flame being invalidated by roof cover made indoor movement unnecessarily destructive and did not match the requested rule. Pumpkin/melon outputs are split by tool purpose: the axe obtains propagation material while the plant cutter obtains edible flesh.

## Tuning snapshot
- Crude Plant Torch carried lifetime: unchanged at 5 minutes.
- Placed torch sky requirement: unchanged.
- Flint Axe gourd output: 1 seed.
- Flint Plant Cutter-tier gourd output: 1 edible slice.
- Melon Slice: 1 nutrition point (0.5 hunger), 0.5 effective saturation.
- Pumpkin Slice: 1 nutrition point (0.5 hunger), 0.5 effective saturation.
- Cooked Chicken: 3 nutrition points (1.5 hunger).
- Cooked Mutton: 5 nutrition points (2.5 hunger).
- Cooked Rabbit: 2 nutrition points (1 hunger), 3.6 effective saturation.
- Cooked Horse Meat: 5 nutrition points (2.5 hunger).

## Assets and licenses
`pumpkin_slice.png` is the exact user-provided PNG from this release request. It is copied byte-for-byte; no pixel edits, resizing, cropping, recoloring, or generated replacement were performed.

## Advancement coverage
No new advancement gate is introduced. These changes refine already-reachable food/tool interactions.

## Known limitations
Minecraft stores nutrition as whole food points. Halving raw foods with odd nutrition values therefore requires integer rounding; this release rounds those hunger values upward while tuning saturation so the previous effective saturation is halved.

## Validation
Release JSON/resources and explicit v0.28.7.8-a invariants are checked by `tools/validate_release.py`. A full Gradle build is attempted separately when the local Loom plugin cache permits it.
