# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.14 — Physical Stockpiles, Finer Stone, and Crude Stone Cutters

## Intent

Push the primitive-material loop forward while fixing the physical readability and inventory-interaction problems found during alpha.13/early alpha.14 testing.

This release makes primitive materials behave more like physical objects: Rocks and Very Refined Stone can be stockpiled, ground assemblies can be dismantled safely, Crushed Stone becomes a refinement surface, world-item pickup respects the five-slot carrying rule, and the first crude cutting tool converts vegetation directly into later progression materials.

## Player-facing behavior

### Crude Stone Hammer presentation, durability, and combat stats

- The Crude Stone Hammer uses normal Minecraft handheld item transforms rather than presenting like a flat/generated inventory sprite in-hand.
- It has 24 native durability and a visible durability bar.
- It uses standard Minecraft item attribute components rather than a custom combat system.
- Its effective main-hand attack values are 2.0 total attack damage and 0.8 attack speed.
- The 2.0 total damage is half the total attack damage of a vanilla Wooden Sword.

### Primitive world-item pickup

- Survival item entities are routed directly through Project Flint's five legal primitive inventory slots.
- A world stack is picked up only when the full stack can fit under the primitive inventory policy.
- If it cannot fit, the item remains in the world rather than being silently moved toward a locked inventory slot or deleted.
- Creative and Spectator pickup behavior remains vanilla.

### Reversible ground materials

- Alpha.13 Fiber, Hammer, and Binding assemblies can be dismantled one component at a time.
- Alpha.14 stockpiles and the cutters assembly follow the same reversible-ground-interaction rule.
- Recovery occurs only when the returned item can fit in the five legal primitive inventory slots.
- Components are never deleted simply because the inventory is full.

### Rock stockpiles

- Ordinary Rocks can be right-clicked onto valid ground.
- A Rock starts a low physical Rock stockpile.
- Additional Rocks can be added up to 12.
- Rocks can be recovered one at a time, allowing early-game stockpiling without introducing chests or backpacks.
- The pile visually changes as its count grows.

### Very Refined Stone

- Right-click Crushed Stone while holding a Rock.
- The held Rock is transformed into one Very Refined Stone.
- Crushed Stone remains in place as the refinement surface.
- Very Refined Stone stacks to 8.
- Very Refined Stone can be placed into a physical 1–8 stockpile and recovered one at a time.

### Crude Stone Cutters assembly

Place and stage:

- 2 Very Refined Stone;
- 2 Plant Tool Bindings;
- 1 Lightly Burnt Twig.

Work the completed arrangement with the Crude Stone Hammer to produce one **Crude Stone Cutters**. Completing the assembly costs one hammer durability.

The incomplete assembly is reversible.

For compatibility with the alpha.14 resource/save namespace, the internal item ID remains `projectflint:crude_harvesting_tool` and the implementation class remains `CrudeHarvestingToolItem`; only the player-facing name is Crude Stone Cutters.

### Crude Stone Cutters stats and harvesting

- The cutters use normal Minecraft handheld item transforms.
- They have 48 native durability and a visible durability bar.
- Their effective main-hand attack values are 1.0 total attack damage and 1.2 attack speed.
- Their damage is half the Crude Stone Hammer's total attack damage, while their attack cadence is modestly faster.
- Breaking Short Grass with the cutters removes the block and yields 1 Crude Plant String.
- Breaking any block in Minecraft's flower tag with the cutters removes the flower and yields 1 Flower Petals.
- Each Short Grass or flower harvest costs one cutter durability.
- These custom harvesting paths do not restore the corresponding vanilla block drops.

## Architecture

- `RockPileBlock` stores physical Rock count state for recoverable 1–12 stockpiles.
- `VeryRefinedStonePileBlock` provides recoverable 1–8 Very Refined Stone stockpiles.
- `HarvestingToolAssemblyBlock` stages the two-stone/two-binding/one-burnt-twig physical assembly.
- `RockItem` handles Rock placement and the Rock-on-Crushed-Stone refinement interaction.
- `CrudeHarvestingToolItem` supplies the native 48-use durability presentation for the player-facing Crude Stone Cutters.
- `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin` owns the cutters' Short Grass/flower conversion behavior and charges native durability for those operations.
- `ItemEntityMixin` routes survival item-entity pickup through `PrimitiveInventorySystem` before vanilla can place a stack into locked inventory space.
- `ModItems` assigns hammer/cutters attack damage and attack speed through standard Minecraft `ItemAttributeModifiers` components.

The combat attributes are deliberately normal Minecraft item data. Project Flint does not introduce a separate combat-stat framework for these primitive tools.

## Design reasoning

### Stockpiles

Project Flint's five-slot inventory is supposed to make carrying capacity a technological problem, not force players to litter item entities everywhere. Early physical stockpiles provide a primitive storage answer without granting conventional containers prematurely.

### Pickup alignment

The five-slot carrying rule must apply consistently regardless of whether an item came from a custom reward path or an ordinary world item entity. Routing pickup through the same primitive inventory boundary prevents locked-slot behavior from undermining the carrying system.

### Very Refined Stone

Crushed Stone was originally only an exhausted visual state. Using that surface to improve loose stone creates a reason to retain it and connects the hammer's first Stone operation to the next tool stage.

### Crude Stone Cutters

The cutters are the first deliberately constructed vegetation-processing tool. They make Short Grass productive without collapsing the earlier hand-clearing stage, and Flower Petals establish a distinct gathered material for later progression.

Native Minecraft durability and attack attributes make the tool readable and predictable to players while avoiding unnecessary custom combat code.

## Tuning snapshot

- Crude Stone Hammer durability: 24.
- Crude Stone Hammer total attack damage: 2.0.
- Crude Stone Hammer attack speed: 0.8.
- Rock stockpile capacity: 12.
- Very Refined Stone stack size: 8.
- Very Refined Stone stockpile capacity: 8.
- Crude Stone Cutters assembly: 2 Very Refined Stone + 2 Plant Tool Bindings + 1 Lightly Burnt Twig.
- Assembly completion cost: 1 Crude Stone Hammer durability.
- Crude Stone Cutters durability: 48.
- Crude Stone Cutters total attack damage: 1.0.
- Crude Stone Cutters attack speed: 1.2.
- Short Grass output: 1 Crude Plant String and 1 cutter durability.
- Flower-tag output: 1 Flower Petals and 1 cutter durability.

No unrelated grass, bush, animal, hunger, worldgen, knapping, or primitive inventory-capacity tuning is changed.

## Assets and licenses

Alpha.14 continues using Project Flint-authored temporary art for Very Refined Stone, Flower Petals, the cutters, stockpiles, and physical assembly presentation.

The existing temporary harvesting-tool sprite is now documented under its shipped player-facing name, **Crude Stone Cutters**. Its internal asset path remains unchanged for compatibility.

No third-party art or other externally licensed asset was added by this hardening patch, so `docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md` requires no change.

## Advancement coverage

Alpha.14 includes progression milestones for:

- **Set It Aside** — place a Rock stockpile.
- **Finer Stone** — create Very Refined Stone.
- **A Tool for the Green** — complete the cutters assembly.
- **Fibers at the Source** — obtain Crude Plant String directly from Short Grass with the cutters.
- **Gathered Color** — harvest Flower Petals.

No additional advancement is needed solely for the combat-stat or pickup-hardening corrections because those changes repair/readjust existing capabilities rather than introduce a separate progression milestone.

## Validation

- JSON resources in the hardening patch were checked for valid structure.
- Item registration was reviewed to confirm 24 hammer durability and 48 cutter durability.
- Standard Minecraft attack attribute components were reviewed to confirm effective main-hand values of 2.0 damage / 0.8 speed for the hammer and 1.0 damage / 1.2 speed for the cutters.
- Harvesting paths were reviewed to confirm one durability is charged for each Short Grass or flower conversion.
- The survival item-entity pickup path was reviewed to confirm it uses the five-slot primitive insertion policy and leaves unfit stacks in-world.
- Handheld model transforms are declared for both primitive tools.
- A complete Gradle/Fabric compile was not reached in the artifact environment because the configured Fabric Loom plugin could not be resolved there. Local `build.bat` compilation and a client/server playtest remain release gates.

## Known limitations

- Final art is still pending.
- The internal `crude_harvesting_tool` identifier is retained even though the shipped display name is Crude Stone Cutters; renaming persistent identifiers solely for terminology would create unnecessary compatibility churn.
- The cutters currently support the specific Short Grass and flower interactions needed by this stage. A generalized data-driven `CUT`/`SCRAPE` operation system remains future work.
- The primitive inventory pickup policy requires the full world stack to fit before pickup; partial world-stack pickup is not introduced by this release.
- Final Minecraft/Fabric runtime validation must still be performed locally.

## Next release pressure

Move from these targeted primitive-tool interactions toward the formal tool-operation system, broader plant/wood processing, and later repair/maintenance progression without retuning alpha.14 behavior unless new playtesting explicitly calls for it.
