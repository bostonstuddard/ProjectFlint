# Project Flint 0.31.26-b — Well Structure/Usability Hotfix

## Intent
Fix the Well Pulley UI/progress issues from first-pass testing, make every Well construction material obey its intended recovery tool, and replace the oversized four-post/full-roof upper frame with the compact owner-defined layout while leaving the lower four brick layers untouched.

## Player-facing behavior
- Well status text no longer overlaps output slots.
- Changing the Rope stack changes production speed immediately without visually rewinding or fast-forwarding the current cycle.
- Rope can be broken with anything.
- Well Pulley and Wooden Beams require Flint Axe or Flint Plant Cutters.
- Wooden Beam dismantling still returns the original Beam plus one Wooden Screw.
- Vanilla Bricks and Brick Shingles require Advanced Flint Multitool tier.

## Architecture
The Well continues using `WellMultiblock` as the single structural validator and `WellPulleyBlockEntity` as the production controller. The controller is now six blocks above the 3x3 brick foundation instead of seven. The upper frame validator accepts the requested pattern on either horizontal axis so players can rotate the visual frame 90 degrees without changing mechanics.

Well progress now uses a fixed work target. Effective quality is added as work every server tick; Rope therefore changes the rate of work accumulation instead of changing the target size. Existing saved progress with the previous variable target is normalized once when loaded/ticked.

## Design reasoning
A fixed work target removes the visible progress jump that occurred when Rope changed `progressMax`. Keeping the upper-frame pattern rotationally symmetric at validation time avoids an arbitrary east/west-only build restriction. Recovery gates live in the same attack/break/destroy-progress authority paths used by the rest of Project Flint so client mining feedback and server authorization agree.

## Well structure
The bottom remains unchanged: Y0 is a solid 3x3 Bricks floor and Y1-Y3 are hollow 3x3 Bricks. The upper four layers are now:

```text
Y7: . . .
    S S S
    . . .

Y6: . . .
    B P B
    . . .

Y5: . . .
    B R B
    . . .

Y4: S . S
    B R B
    S . S
```

`S` = Brick Shingles, `B` = Wooden Beam, `P` = Well Pulley, `R` = Rope. The pattern may be rotated 90 degrees.

## Tuning snapshot
- Base Well cycle remains 60 seconds at 100 effective quality.
- Each Rope still adds +2 quality points, up to +32 from a stack of 16.
- Well output buffer remains 8 slots.
- Rope recovery: any tool/hand.
- Well Pulley / Wooden Beam recovery: Flint Axe or Flint Plant Cutters.
- Bricks / Brick Shingles recovery: Advanced Flint Multitool tier.

## Assets and licenses
No new visual assets are introduced by this hotfix. Existing owner-supplied Brick Shingle art and the current placeholder Well Pulley art remain unchanged.

## Advancement coverage
None. Advancements remain retired in favor of Flint Quests.

## Known limitations
- Well Pulley still uses placeholder art because no final pulley texture has been supplied yet.
- The Well must still be centered on the exact virtual aquifer center column.
- Brick recovery gating applies to vanilla Bricks globally, not only Bricks placed as part of a Well.

## Validation
The release validator checks the new six-block controller offset, both upper-frame orientations, fixed-work progress model, reflowed GUI slots/status text, Rope/Pulley/Beam/Brick/Shingle recovery gates, release versioning, and prior release regressions.

## Next release pressure
Continue Well playtesting, especially structure readability and production balance across Scarce/Normal/Good/Abundant aquifers, then replace the Well Pulley placeholder once final art is supplied.
