# Project Flint v0.31.47-b — Hardwood balance and Field Guide

## Intent
Correct the unintended Hammer strip-mining advantage, make Hardwood tools feel properly tiered and functional, and give players an accessible in-game manual explaining the overhaul without requiring external wikis or Flint Quests.

## Player-facing behavior
- Hardwood Hammer mines eligible natural stone and pickaxe-mineable ores at a fixed 21s/block instead of the unintended roughly 4.75s fast-path (1/95 progress plus vanilla max). No fast vanilla mining override; retaining stone-tier/lower ores, including fish-killing melee utility.
- Hammer combat properties significantly lowered; includes the tooltip "Can injure and kill fish." alongside a mining note.
- Wooden Pickaxe stone/coal/crushed stone remains 13.5s; Hardwood Pickaxe now 9.0s (33% faster), masonry 6s vs 4.5s. Hardwood durability remains 92 and no new iron/diamond tier access is granted.
- Hardwood Axe is a real Minecraft AxeItem, supporting vanilla right-click stripping of logs/wood and receiving normal wear.
- Default **G** opens the new Field Guide from gameplay. Default **K** keeps opening the existing recipe browser. Hotkeys may be rebound through Minecraft Controls. Handbook is usable from the moment the player spawns and needs no inventory item or special armor.
- The guide has 25 navigation chapters covering the startup, stone, fire, clay, kiln, survival, Wood/Water, Forge and Hardwood progression. Multiple pages explain mechanics, equipment and new content. Search accepts topic/item names. Item Atlas automatically enumerates and paginates all registered ProjectFlint items and block-items, displays icons/names/IDs and full hover tooltips. Recipes (K) opens directly from the Field Guide and returns to it upon closing.

## Architecture
- The `BlockStateBaseMixin` Hammer progress branch applies a fixed `1/420` progress per tick to permitted pickaxe-tagged targets; it runs before vest/player-placed preview and does not use `Math.max` with vanilla mining speed. All three previously introduced server-side mining/loot gates are retained.
- `ModItems.HARDWOOD_AXE` is an `AxeItem` with the same material/attributes; `AxeItem.useOn` handles stripping normally. The registration helper continues to register it as before.
- Client-only `ProjectFlintFieldGuideScreen` and `ProjectFlintFieldGuideContent` do not register recipes or touch server/gameplay. Content chapters are authored; item atlas is sourced from live `BuiltInRegistries.ITEM`. Client tick keybinding uses Fabric's existing keybinding API.

## Design reasoning
A hard Hammer should be good at specialty ores and as a tool of last resort, not better than a purpose-built Pickaxe at stone. Giving the Hardwood Pickaxe a clearly measurable benefit rewards progression without making the world trivial to mine. A broad, searchable manual is immediately helpful even before the player is able to craft a physical book.

## Tuning snapshot
| Operation | Wooden Pickaxe | Hardwood Pickaxe | Hardwood Hammer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stone/Coal Ore/Crushed Stone custom target | 270 ticks / 13.5s | 180 ticks / 9s | 420 ticks / 21s |
| Brick masonry | 120 ticks / 6s | 90 ticks / 4.5s | 420 ticks / 21s when pickaxe-tagged (existing explicit masonry handlers also apply) |
| Durability | Vanilla 59 | 92 | 196 |
| Item attack modifier | unchanged | unchanged | 2.0 (was 8.0) |
| Tool material attack bonus | unchanged | 0.5 | 1.0 (was 3.0) |

## Assets and licenses
No external assets used or generated. Leather and parchment appearance uses colored GUI fills and the existing approved registered item icons; the original Hardwood item textures and models are preserved intact.

## Advancement coverage
The standalone Flint Quests mod retains authority over tracked progression. This manual is a reference UI only and does not add Minecraft advancement registrations or reintroduce the old ProjectFlint EMI plugin.

## Known limitations
- In-game GUI screenshots and the full Fabric Gradle build require the user's Minecraft build environment; the offline verification here compiles the new manual screens against 1.21.11 mapped classes using temporary stubs for unavailable auxiliary dependencies.
- Item Atlas shows all registered ProjectFlint items, including creative/debug/staged objects, not only items reachable in Survival.
- The handbook is a fixed curated explanation of gameplay mechanics, while item names/icons come from the live registry. It is not a substitute for generated recipe browsing (use K).
- Small GUI sizes may require scrolling long text inside the right page; scroll navigation over the left index to move chapters.

## Validation
1. Mine Stone with Wooden Pickaxe, Hardwood Pickaxe and Hardwood Hammer, both with and without a Utility Vest. Verify 270, 180 and 420 ticks respectively; tool enchantments/effects may influence effective timings elsewhere.
2. Break Stone-tier Ore with the Hammer and check drops; try Iron-/Diamond-tier ores beyond its access tier. Verify strict prohibitions still apply.
3. Strike fish with the Hammer and compare attack damage against the prior overtuned build; inspect tooltips for fish-related description.
4. Right-click ordinary vanilla Logs and Wood with Hardwood Axe, verify stripped block state, sound and durability wear.
5. Press G with no tools/armor, navigate every Field Guide chapter using sidebar, prev/next, scrolling and text search; open and close Recipe Guide with internal Recipes button; search for a newly added item in Atlas; verify icon hover tooltips.
6. Run source validator and Java compiler, plus a fresh Fabric client/runtime test; this release does not claim client runtime or full Gradle success.
