# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.22.4

## Intent

Extend the custom alpha.22 progression with reusable filing, bone tools, portable rest, mortar masonry, and a general supported-item placement action while preserving the existing Grinder, processing, salvage, capability, and texture boundaries.

## Player-facing behavior

- Reusable Sanding Paper files every vanilla dye-producing flower into its normal vanilla dye output, files Animal Bones or vanilla Bones into Filed Bone, and files 2 Project Flint Wool into 1 Woven Wool.
- Filed Bone worked with offhand Flint creates a 96-use Bone Firestarter. The Clay Grinder processes 4 Filed Bones into a Bone Club with 4.2 total attack damage and 1 attack speed.
- Placing 8 Woven Wool together creates a supported one-block Crude Sleeping Bag. Any vanilla dye recolors it; breaking it returns exactly 2 Woven Wool. Sleeping through the night grants Regeneration I for 2 seconds.
- The configurable **Place in World** key defaults to `V`. It places one held item flat on sturdy support; right-click reclaims it, attacks do nothing, and lost support causes a normal world drop.
- The Clay Grinder processes 9 Super Heated Clay into Clay Mortar. Water mixing yields 4 Unfired Mortar Bricks, and Kiln firing creates non-gravity Mortar Bricks.
- Below 7 hearts the player is Weakened with Slowness I. Below 4 hearts the player is Dying with Slowness II, Mining Fatigue I, and Weakness I. Below 4 hunger shanks the player is Hungry, cannot jump, and receives Weakness I; Dying plus Hungry becomes Weakness II. HUD labels show the active state.
- Chickens have 20% less health than the previous Project Flint passive-health result. Cow Leather is replaced by Raw Hide while the existing Animal Bones drop remains.
- Twig → Burnt Twig and Branch → Burnt Branch now use zero processing ticks. Sneak-placed Burnt Branches have a dedicated assembly model texture.

## Architecture

Filing extends `StoneWorkingSystem.Process`, `PrimitiveProgressPayload`, and `PrimitiveHud`; `PrimitiveInteractionSystem` owns the final atomic transactions. `ToolCapabilities.FIRESTARTER` centralizes ignition inheritance. The fully custom Clay Grinder receives two additional `projectflint:clay_grinding` recipes without vanilla stonecutter inheritance.

`PlacedItemSystem` is server-authoritative and creates a tagged vanilla `Interaction` holder plus an `ItemDisplay` passenger after rechecking range, raycast, support, and space. The Crude Sleeping Bag is a supported custom block with `FACING` and `DyeColor` state. `PrimitiveConditionSystem`, `PlayerJumpMixin`, and `ServerPlayerSleepMixin` own survival and rest effects. Mortar firing stays in the shared one-item `PrimitiveProcessingRegistry` as a kiln-only recipe.

## Design reasoning

Reusable Sanding Paper remains a capability object rather than a durability sink. New fire ignition inherits the centralized Firestarter capability. The placed-item feature uses generic vanilla display/interaction entities so every item can be represented without creating one block type per item. Mortar follows the established Grinder → water interaction → Kiln progression and becomes the requested first non-gravity building block.

## Assets and licenses

Crushed Stone, Crude Clay Block, Mortar Bricks, Filed Bone, Bone Firestarter, Bone Club, Woven Wool, Clay Mortar, Unfired Mortar Brick, and Raw Hide use exact byte-for-byte human-made PNGs from the supplied `ProjectFlint - Textures/texture asset master file for use/` archive. No pixels were generated, redrawn, recolored, resized, cropped, composited, or filtered. Sleeping Bag models reference existing vanilla wool textures. Exact hashes and source paths are recorded in both provenance ledgers.

## Advancement coverage

New branches cover Reed Roll, Sugarcane Roll, Sanding Paper, filed flower dyes, Filed Bone, Bone Firestarter, Bone Club, Woven Wool, Crude Sleeping Bag, Clay Mortar, Unfired Mortar Bricks, Mortar Bricks, and Place in World.

## Validation

The release validator checks version/document markers, all JSON and linked resources, Grinder recipe counts, balance constants, processing rules, item/block/model/advancement coverage, mixin registration, and exact texture-provenance hashes. A Gradle compile was attempted here but stopped before Java compilation because Fabric Loom 1.14.10 could not be resolved in this environment. No full build pass is claimed; run `build.bat` and a first-client-launch smoke test locally before publishing.

## Known limitations

- Placed items are runtime entities and are intentionally limited to a flat supported presentation; they do not expose inventory, rotation, or other interactions.
- The one-block Sleeping Bag uses a compact wool model rather than vanilla two-block bed geometry.
- Survival-condition effects refresh in short server-authoritative intervals, so a threshold recovery can leave at most a brief effect tail.
