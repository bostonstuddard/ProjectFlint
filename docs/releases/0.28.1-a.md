# Project Flint v0.28.1-a

## Loose soil
- Loose Soil is included in the vanilla dirt block tag so multitool mining treats it like Dirt.
- Neighbor conversion chance reduced from 50% to 10%.

## Kilns
- Crude and Baked Clay Kiln model geometry is unchanged; only their material texture source now uses Crude Clay Block.
- Active Advanced Kiln controllers now propagate their active/light state to all seven casing blocks, lighting the full 2x2x2 structure.

## Bears
- Added Black Bear and Brown Bear variants using the supplied 128x64 textures unchanged.
- Black Bear: rarer moderate forest/taiga/meadow spawns; normally flees nearby players, but inherited protective/retaliation behavior remains available around cubs or after being attacked.
- Brown Bear: rarer rough/mountain/old-growth terrain spawns and aggressively targets players inside 8 blocks.
- Vanilla Polar Bears now also proactively target players inside 8 blocks and move 25% faster.
- Black/Brown Bear drops: 1-2 Bear Meat and 2-3 Animal Bones.
- Their health/damage sits above the modded Cow tier but below the modded Polar Bear tier.

## Environmental debuffs
- Added Soaked while standing/swimming in water or exposed to rain.
- Soaked applies Slowness I + Hunger I behavior and halves hunger-based natural healing.
- Added a creative-tab-only Splash Potion of Soaking using Minecraft's normal splash-potion item renderer.
- Added Terrified below light level 5: Blindness I, Mining Fatigue I, and Weakness I.
- After 15 continuous seconds in qualifying darkness, Terrified begins periodic wither-style damage and can be lethal.
