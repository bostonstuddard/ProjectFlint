# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.13 — First Material Processing

## Intent
Push Project Flint beyond the opening forage/fire loop into the first real material-processing chain. The player should begin combining loose primitive materials in the world, produce the first reusable processing tool, gain controlled access to reeds and stone, and finish with a purpose-built Plant Tool Binding for the next tool stage.

This release deliberately keeps the interaction physical: components are placed on the ground, visibly accumulate, and are worked with the appropriate item instead of being combined through the disabled vanilla crafting grid.

## Player-facing behavior

### Fire ignition cost
- Every completed attempt to light a full 12-Twig Pile now consumes the four held Twigs.
- This applies to both success and failure.
- The Refined Rock used as the striking stone remains unchanged.
- A successful pile still burns for 10 seconds and can still convert clicked Twigs into Lightly Burnt Twigs.

### Crude Plant String
- Plant Fiber is now placeable as a low ground pile.
- A second Plant Fiber can be added to the same position.
- Striking a complete two-fiber pile with a Refined Rock consumes the Refined Rock and both fibers, producing one Crude Plant String.
- Empty-hand interaction can recover fibers before the process is completed, subject to the primitive five-slot carrying limit.

### Crude Stone Hammer
- Lightly Burnt Twigs can now be placed as a physical ground assembly.
- A complete hammer assembly requires two Lightly Burnt Twigs and one ordinary Rock.
- Striking that complete assembly with a Refined Rock consumes the Refined Rock and all placed components, producing one Crude Stone Hammer.
- The Crude Stone Hammer has 24 durability for Project Flint processing operations.

### Crude Reed harvesting
- Sugar Cane can no longer be treated as an ordinary primitive break target.
- With a Crude Stone Hammer it becomes a deliberately slow harvest operation, using the same break duration as hand-stripping a Grass Block: 156 ticks.
- Cane segments removed by the completed operation produce Crude Reeds instead of vanilla Sugar Cane.
- Hammer durability is consumed for harvested cane segments.

### Stone crushing
- A Crude Stone Hammer can work normal Stone.
- Stone takes 234 ticks to complete: exactly 1.5x the established 156-tick hand Grass operation.
- Completion produces four Loose Rocks and replaces the Stone with `projectflint:crushed_stone`.
- Crushed Stone intentionally has no primitive follow-up operation yet. It records the worked-out stone face visually and prevents repeatedly extracting rocks from the same block.
- One hammer durability is consumed per completed Stone operation.

### Plant Tool Binding
- Crude Reeds can be placed as another low ground assembly.
- The completed arrangement requires four Crude Reeds and two Crude Plant Strings.
- Striking the complete arrangement with a Crude Stone Hammer consumes the six placed components, uses one hammer durability, and produces one Plant Tool Binding.
- Empty-hand interaction can recover placed components before completion, subject to primitive inventory capacity.

## Architecture introduced
Three focused stateful blocks establish the first reusable player-facing language for physical assembly:

- `FiberWorkPileBlock`
- `HammerAssemblyBlock`
- `BindingAssemblyBlock`

`PrimitiveInteractionSystem` routes direct strike interactions into those assemblies. `BlockStateBaseMixin` and `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin` extend the existing primitive destruction pipeline with the two first tool-driven block operations: Sugar Cane harvesting and Stone crushing.

`Crushed Stone` is a real registered block with no loot table and no current primitive operation. It is a material-state marker rather than a new resource source.

ADR `0009-physical-ground-assembly.md` records why the first few processes remain narrow physical blocks instead of prematurely introducing a generic crafting engine.

## Design reasoning
The key progression change is not merely adding new items. It establishes three new capabilities:

1. **material preparation** — Plant Fiber can be transformed into a structural string;
2. **reusable processing tools** — a limited-durability hammer can perform work that bare hands and loose rocks cannot;
3. **persistent world transformation** — worked Stone visibly remains as an exhausted Crushed Stone state instead of disappearing into an inventory block.

This keeps the world legible. A player can look at an area and see where Stone has already been worked rather than treating every block as a renewable four-Rock dispenser.

Ground assemblies also preserve the five-slot inventory pressure: the player must physically stage materials rather than carrying an unlimited crafting inventory.

## Tuning snapshot
- Twig ignition input: 4 held Twigs consumed on every completed attempt.
- Twig ignition success: 50%.
- Twig fire duration: 200 ticks / 10 seconds.
- Plant Fiber processing: 2 Plant Fiber + 1 consumed Refined Rock -> 1 Crude Plant String.
- Hammer assembly: 2 Lightly Burnt Twigs + 1 Rock + 1 consumed Refined Rock -> 1 Crude Stone Hammer.
- Crude Stone Hammer durability: 24 operations/durability points.
- Sugar Cane harvest duration: 156 ticks.
- Stone crushing duration: 234 ticks.
- Stone crushing output: 4 Rocks.
- Plant Tool Binding: 4 Crude Reeds + 2 Crude Plant String + hammer work -> 1 Plant Tool Binding.

No accepted Grass, bush, animal, wolf, hunger, inventory-capacity, worldgen, or knapping tuning is changed by this release.

## Advancement coverage
This release adds advancement coverage for every major new capability:

- **Twisted by Hand** — create Crude Plant String.
- **Stone on a Handle** — create the Crude Stone Hammer.
- **Reed Harvest** — harvest Crude Reeds.
- **Breaking Ground** — work Stone into Crushed Stone and recover rocks.
- **A Stronger Binding** — create a Plant Tool Binding.

The advancement tree remains descriptive of achieved capabilities; none of these advancements acts as an artificial unlock flag.

## Known limitations
- The three ground assemblies are intentionally process-specific prototypes. They should be consolidated only after several more physical processes establish what the generic system actually needs.
- Crushed Stone has no recovery or further processing path yet by design.
- Cane harvesting currently converts the targeted cane segment and contiguous cane above it into Crude Reeds; ecological regrowth/replanting will be designed later.
- The Crude Stone Hammer currently receives durability loss only from Project Flint processing operations covered by this stage.
- The Crude Stone Hammer is introduced here as a processing tool; specialized combat behavior is outside this release scope.

## Next pressure
The Plant Tool Binding is intentionally an endpoint for this release and an input for the next tool-development slice. The next stage should use the new string/binding/reed materials to introduce the first deliberate cutting/scraping capability without reopening vanilla crafting or granting full tree harvesting prematurely.
