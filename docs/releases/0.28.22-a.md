# Project Flint v0.28.22-a — Era Guidance & Recipe Reference

## Intent
Make the now-large Project Flint progression understandable in-game without turning advancements into walls of documentation.

## Player-facing behavior
- Advancements are split into three independent tabs: **Tool Era**, **Clay Era**, and **Wood Era**.
- The old all-in-one Project Flint tree is retired.
- Forty redundant, duplicated, or overly granular milestones were removed from the visible advancement set.
- Remaining advancement descriptions are short one-sentence instructions aimed at telling the player what to try next.
- The survival inventory recipe-book button now opens a Project Flint station recipe guide instead of vanilla crafting recipes.
- Clay Grinder, Wooden Grinder, and Advanced Kiln screens also include a **Recipes** button.
- The guide browses Permanent Campfire, Baked Kiln, Mortar Kiln, Clay Grinder, Wooden Grinder, and Advanced Kiln recipes.

## Architecture
- Added `ProjectFlintRecipeGuideScreen` as the shared client recipe-reference UI.
- Added `WoodenGrinderRecipeRegistry` and moved Wooden Grinder dual-input matching into that shared source of truth.
- `PrimitiveProcessingRegistry` now exposes processor-specific recipe lists for guide rendering.
- Clay Grinder recipes are read from the synchronized client recipe manager instead of duplicated into UI code.
- Advancement item fallback mappings were trimmed to milestones that still exist.

## Design reasoning
Advancements should answer “what should I try next?” while the recipe guide answers “what can this workstation make?”. Separating those jobs keeps the progression readable even as the number of items and workstation recipes grows.

## Tuning snapshot
- Tool Era: immediate root; ends at the Flint Multitool milestone.
- Clay Era: unlocked by obtaining a Flint Multitool.
- Wood Era: unlocked by obtaining a Clay Grinder.
- Recipe guide: 5 recipes per page with station and page navigation.

## Assets and licenses
No new texture pixels, models, audio, or third-party assets were introduced by this release. The recipe guide is code-rendered using existing item models.

## Advancement coverage
The curated tree keeps major progression actions and removes duplicate item-state milestones, one-off food milestones, repetitive mold milestones, and other details better communicated through the recipe guide or normal gameplay feedback.

## Known limitations
- The recipe guide shows an example item for tag/multi-item ingredients rather than cycling every valid ingredient.
- Wooden Grinder single-input recipes intentionally mirror the Clay Grinder list because the Wooden Grinder supports those recipes too.
- Recipe entries are currently visible regardless of whether the player has discovered that recipe or station.

## Validation
- All advancement/resource JSON files parse successfully.
- Every non-root advancement has an existing parent in the curated tree.
- Exactly three Project Flint advancement roots are present.
- Removed advancement IDs are listed in the v0.28.22-a cleanup BAT so upgrading source does not leave the old tree behind.
- Shared Wooden Grinder dual recipes are used by both gameplay matching and the recipe guide.

## Next release pressure
As more eras are introduced, keep tabs technology-focused and avoid reintroducing an advancement for every new item; new station recipes should be added to the shared guide at the same time as their gameplay implementation.
