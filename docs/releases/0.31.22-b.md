# Project Flint 0.31.22-b

## Intent
Repair the Advanced Kiln Crude Clay Block process, move chicken egg production behind a nest requirement, and clean the pause-menu row left behind by the removal of Minecraft advancements.

## Player-facing behavior
- The Advanced Kiln material slot now accepts up to 8 Unfired Clay Balls, including shift-click routing, so the Fired Clay Block Mold recipe can actually start.
- Adult non-jockey chickens only lay eggs when a usable Chicken Nest is within 10 blocks.
- A Chicken Nest stores up to 4 eggs and becomes owned by the first chicken that successfully lays in it.
- Empty-hand right click removes one egg from the nest. Breaking the nest has no special tool requirement and drops its stored eggs plus the nest item.
- Chicken Nests are creative-only for now.
- When Flint Quests is installed, the retired Advancements pause-menu slot becomes a Quests button. Without Flint Quests, Statistics expands across the full row.

## Architecture
Chicken egg gating is implemented by a small server-side Chicken mixin that intercepts the moment an adult chicken becomes ready to lay. Nest lookup/storage lives in Project Flint-owned classes and persistent block-entity data. Flint Quests pause-menu support uses Fabric Loader detection plus reflection so Project Flint does not gain a hard compile/runtime dependency on Flint Quests.

## Design reasoning
The Advanced Kiln already had the correct process definition, but its menu only accepted Fired Clay and capped the shared material slot at four items. Fixing the GUI constraint keeps the existing kiln process intact. The nest mechanic deliberately stores eggs instead of spawning item entities, making the nest the actual source of chicken eggs. Reflection is used for the optional Quests button so independently packaged Flint Quests builds remain optional.

## Tuning snapshot
- Nest search radius: 10 blocks.
- Nest capacity: 4 eggs.
- Failed nest lookup retry: 200 ticks / 10 seconds.
- Successful chicken lay cooldown: vanilla 6000-11999 tick range.
- Advanced Kiln Crude Clay Block material input: 8 Unfired Clay Balls.

## Assets and licenses
No external texture assets were added. The temporary Chicken Nest block model uses Minecraft's existing hay-bale textures.

## Advancement coverage
Minecraft/Project Flint advancements remain fully retired. Flint Quests is the intended progression replacement.

## Known limitations
Chicken Nests are creative-only and use a simple temporary model. Nest ownership persists until the block is broken; there is not yet a dedicated ownership-management UI.

## Validation
The release validator checks the kiln slot capacity/type routing, Chicken Nest registration/storage/ownership/radius logic, chicken egg interception, pause-menu Flint Quests detection, and absence of Project Flint advancement JSONs. JSON resources are parsed as part of validation.

## Next release pressure
Replace the temporary nest acquisition path with progression content once its intended crafting/material chain is designed, and continue expanding Flint Quests integration for bespoke Project Flint mechanics.
