# Project Flint v0.28.30-a — Wood → Water Age, Part 1

## Intent
Begin the transition from late Wood Era handcrafting into water-dependent infrastructure by adding precision surface processing, finite water transport, and the first dedicated lumber-processing multiblock.

## Player-facing behavior
The Wooden Grinder can now create a Sanding Station from 8 Sanded Wood Slabs and 5 Leather. Sanding Paper is loaded directly into the station: one sheet adds 32 sanding durability, the station stores up to 256, and every completed recipe spends one point without damaging the station itself.

The Sanding Station processes 2 Plant Tool Bindings → 3 Woven Fans, 4 Glass Shards → Advanced Kiln Glass, 3 Raw Leather → 2 Leather, 2 Animal Bones → 3 Filed Bone, 2 non-Nether wooden slabs → 3 Sanded Wood Slabs, and 3 Project Flint Wool → 2 Woven Wool.

The Wooden Grinder also gains 16 Woven Wood + Clay Pot → Water Bearing Pot, 4 Leather + Clay Pot → Water Pot, and Flint Axe Pieces + 2 Leather → Bound Wood Processor. A Water Bearing Pot carries exactly half a bucket of finite surface water. Two pours fill a Water Pot to its one-bucket capacity.

A Lumber Processor forms from Woven Wood → Water Pot → Crushed Stone in a horizontal three-block row. Each base cell must remain on a solid support and the cell above each must be clear when formed. Using the Bound Wood Processor on the Woven Wood converts it into the Chopping Block/controller. The controller selects either 2 Wooden Boards or 1 Wooden Beam.

Place a stripped overworld log above the Chopping Block, fill the Water Pot to one bucket, and cut the log with Flint Plant Cutters for 7.5 seconds. The machine washes for 10 seconds, consumes the bucket, and places a half-height unfinished workpiece above the Crushed Stone. Finishing that workpiece takes another 7.5 seconds and drops the selected lumber output.

## Architecture
`SandingStationRecipeRegistry` is the single gameplay/guide definition for Sanding Station recipes. `SandingStationBlockEntity` owns the 0–256 abrasive reserve, while `SandingStationMenu` spends one point only when output is actually taken.

`WaterBearingPotItem` owns half-bucket collection/pouring. It only drains literal vanilla Water blocks, never waterlogged blocks. `WaterPotBlockEntity` stores two half-bucket units and can link to a Lumber Processor controller.

`LumberProcessorSystem` owns formation, placement, validation, recovery, teardown, water consumption, workpiece creation, and output harvesting. `LumberChoppingBlockEntity` owns the selected output and 200-tick wash stage. The finished workpiece uses a dedicated half-height collision/outline shape.

## Design reasoning
The Sanding Station turns Sanding Paper from a one-off ingredient into workstation upkeep and makes precision finishing a distinct manufacturing step. The Lumber Processor deliberately requires both prepared wood technology and finite water, creating a direct bridge toward the Water Age rather than granting better wood products through another simple recipe.

The complete machine can be recovered only with Advanced Flint-tier technology so the multiblock remains meaningful infrastructure while still being relocatable once the player has earned the necessary tool quality.

## Tuning snapshot
- Sanding Station capacity: **256 durability**
- Sanding Paper charge: **32 durability**
- Sanding cost: **1 durability / recipe**
- Water Bearing Pot: **0.5 bucket**
- Water Pot: **1.0 bucket**
- Lumber chop: **7.5 seconds**
- Lumber wash: **10 seconds**
- Workpiece finishing: **7.5 seconds**
- Board output: **2 Wooden Boards**
- Beam output: **1 Wooden Beam**
- Supported input wood: stripped Overworld logs only; Nether stems are excluded.

## Assets and licenses
No new third-party or generated texture pixels were added. The Sanding Station, Water Pots, Bound Wood Processor, Lumber Processor, Wooden Boards/Beam, Chopping Block, and workpiece intentionally point at missing/null placeholder art until the owner supplies finalized textures.

## Advancement coverage
Wood Era gains granular mini-guide steps for building/loading the Sanding Station, making/filling both water vessels, creating the Bound Wood Processor, forming the Lumber Processor, beginning the wash process, and obtaining Wooden Boards or a Wooden Beam.

## Known limitations
This is only Water Age part 1. The Well multiblock and renewable aquifer water production are intentionally not implemented here. New art is intentionally absent. Full Gradle assembly cannot be validated in the current sandbox when Fabric Loom 1.14.10 is not locally resolvable.

## Validation
Resource JSON is syntax-checked by the release validator. The validator also checks sanding durability/costs, recipe membership and Nether-wood exclusion, water capacities, multiblock composition, 7.5/10/7.5-second processing timings, Advanced Flint recovery, and advancement coverage.

## Next release pressure
Add owner-supplied Wood → Water textures, then connect the Water Pot/Water Bearing Pot progression into the future virtual-aquifer Well. The next machinery step should build on Wooden Boards/Beams instead of bypassing the Lumber Processor with direct crafting recipes.
