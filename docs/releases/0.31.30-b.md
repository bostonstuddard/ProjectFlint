# Project Flint 0.31.30-b

## Intent
Add Strawberries as a small temperate-foraging expansion that behaves consistently with Project Flint's existing berry/tool rules while using the supplied Strawberry artwork directly.

## Player-facing behavior
- Strawberry is an edible, plantable item with a stack size of 16, 1 nutrition, and 0.15 saturation modifier.
- Placing a Strawberry on valid ground plants an age-0 Strawberry Plant.
- Strawberry Plants have four visual/growth stages, age 0 through age 3. They inherit Sweet Berry Bush-style random ticking and bone-meal growth, but they do not apply thorn movement slowdown or damage.
- In Survival, age 2 or age 3 plants require a Project Flint plant-cutting tool to harvest. A successful harvest yields 1–2 Strawberries, resets the plant to age 1, and damages the harvesting tool by 1 durability.
- Flint Plant Cutters or the Advanced Flint Multitool can uproot the whole Strawberry Plant and recover one Strawberry for replanting.
- Natural Strawberry patches can generate in Birch Forest, Old Growth Birch Forest, Flower Forest, Forest, and Meadow biomes.

## Architecture
`StrawberryBushBlock` extends Minecraft's `SweetBerryBushBlock`, retaining the four-stage growth property and growth mechanics while overriding clone/creative harvest behavior and removing thorn effects. Survival interaction authority remains in Project Flint's existing `PrimitiveInteractionSystem`, destroy-progress mixin, and server game-mode harvest path, matching the existing Sweet Berry Bush restrictions. World generation is data-driven through `strawberry_patch` configured/placed features and is attached to the selected biomes through `ModWorldgen`.

## Design reasoning
Strawberries should feel like another naturally discoverable forage plant rather than a new workstation chain. Reusing the established Project Flint berry interaction rules keeps early cutters useful and prevents ordinary bare-hand berry harvesting from bypassing progression. The plant is intentionally non-thorny so the Sweet Berry Bush implementation is reused for growth without importing its defensive behavior.

## Tuning snapshot
- Strawberry stack size: 16.
- Food: 1 nutrition, 0.15 saturation modifier.
- Growth stages: 0–3.
- Harvestable stages: 2–3.
- Survival fruit harvest: 1–2 Strawberries, reset to age 1, 1 cutter durability.
- Whole-plant recovery: Flint Plant Cutters or Advanced Flint Multitool, 1 Strawberry.
- Natural patch placement: rarity filter 32, 96 tries, X/Z spread 7, Y spread 3, mature age-3 plants, Grass Block support.
- Biomes: Birch Forest, Old Growth Birch Forest, Flower Forest, Forest, Meadow.

## Assets and licenses
The Strawberry item texture and all four Strawberry Plant growth-stage textures are direct user-supplied assets from `new update stuff(6).zip`. They are copied byte-for-byte. Their hashes and source paths are recorded in `docs/TEXTURE_PROVENANCE.md` and `docs/texture_provenance.json`.

## Advancement coverage
Minecraft/Project Flint advancements remain retired. This release adds no advancement hooks; Flint Quests remains the progression/discovery system.

## Known limitations
Natural Strawberry generation only affects newly generated terrain. This release adds the raw Strawberry/plant ecology only; it does not invent jams, pies, workstation recipes, or other processing that was not requested.

## Validation
The release validator checks registration, resources, harvesting gates/yields, growth reset, uprooting rules, world-generation tuning/biome attachment, direct asset hashes, and cleanup/version wiring. Full Gradle build status is recorded separately during packaging and is not assumed from static validation.

## Next release pressure
Future food processing can add Strawberry recipes if desired, but this release intentionally keeps the crop independent and avoids expanding unrelated progression systems.
