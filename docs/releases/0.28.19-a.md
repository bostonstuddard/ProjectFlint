# Project Flint v0.28.19-a

## Intent
Expand the new Wattle-and-Daub shelter tier beyond solid walls while preserving its deliberately limited primitive structural rules. This release adds usable flooring, light-admitting structural windows, and a creative-only door prototype, then fixes two placement/drop issues found during playtesting.

## Player-facing behavior
- One Wattled Daub Brick grinds into two Wattled Daub Floor Tiles in the Clay Grinder or Wooden Grinder. There is intentionally no return recipe.
- Floor Tiles are ordinary support flooring: beds, workstations, chests, and other normal blocks may be placed on them.
- Structural Wattled Daub Bricks/Windows and more Floor Tiles cannot be stacked directly on Floor Tiles.
- Two Wattled Daub Bricks plus one Advanced Kiln Glass in the Wooden Grinder produce one Wattled Daub Window.
- The Window behaves structurally like a normal Wattled Daub Brick, including gravity, four-block height, two-block roof reach, crumble drops, and Flint Multitool intact recovery.
- The Wattled Daub Door is currently creative-only. It must stand on a Wattled Daub Floor Tile and may carry Wattled Daub wall pieces above it.
- The Door occupies two structural cells, so doorway walls remain governed by the same four-cell vertical limit as the rest of the system.
- Crude Plant Torches now display vanilla-style smoke and flame particles while placed.
- Project Flint placeable BlockItems refuse water placement. Dirt Piles still convert to Mud Piles when intentionally used on water.
- Unsupported falling Wattled Daub now drops only its intended crumble materials instead of also duplicating the intact block.

## Architecture
`WattledDaubBricksBlock` remains the structural authority. Its wall-cell helpers now recognize both solid bricks and windows, while a Wattled Daub Door contributes two structural cells only when founded on a Floor Tile. Floor Tiles are deliberately excluded as ordinary wall foundations; the Door is the single structural exception.

`PrimitiveInteractionSystem` owns player-facing placement restrictions. It distinguishes ordinary Floor Tile support from structural walls and also rejects Project Flint BlockItem placement into water before vanilla can consume the stack.

The Wooden Grinder continues to use Clay Grinder single-input recipes, so the Floor Tile 1:2 recipe automatically works in both machines. The Window is a Wooden-Grinder-only two-input recipe in `WoodenGrinderMenu`.

## Design reasoning
Flooring should make primitive shelters livable without becoming a structural exploit. Making the floor a normal support surface allows beds and machines, while explicitly refusing new wall/floor pieces on top prevents it from being used as a hidden extra layer in the four-block wall limit.

The Door is intentionally available only for creative testing until Project Flint reaches the woodworking/joinery technology needed to justify a survival door recipe.

## Tuning snapshot
- Wattled Daub maximum structural height: 4 cells.
- Wattled Daub ceiling reach from a valid four-cell column: 2 blocks horizontally.
- Floor Tile grinder conversion: 1 Brick -> 2 Floor Tiles.
- Window Wooden Grinder conversion: 2 Bricks + 1 Advanced Kiln Glass -> 1 Window.
- Window weak-tool crumble: 1 Crude Reed + 2 Twigs.
- Door survival recipe: none.

## Advancement coverage
- `A Floor Underfoot` — obtain a Wattled Daub Floor Tile.
- `Let There Be Light` — obtain a Wattled Daub Window.
- The creative-only Door intentionally has no survival advancement yet.

## Known limitations
- Wattled Daub Door is a technology preview: no survival recipe and no intact survival recovery are provided yet.
- A fresh Gradle build cannot be completed in the current sandbox because Fabric Loom `1.14.10` is not available in the offline plugin cache.

## Validation
- Resource JSON files are parsed during release validation.
- Asset SHA-256 checks verify the five supplied PNGs remain byte-identical.
- Release validation checks Floor Tile/Window/Door registration, structural inclusion, recipes, water-placement guard, torch particles, and the early FallingBlock drop disable.
- Full Gradle compilation remains blocked by unavailable Fabric Loom plugin resolution in this environment.
