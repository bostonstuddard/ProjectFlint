# Project Flint v0.31.66-b — Dynamite and Stone Processing

## New items and recipes

- Fuse-Ready Dust: Silicon Dust + Aluminum Dust + Tin Dust + Silver Dust in the Hardwood Crafting Table.
- Fine Fuse Powder: heat Fuse-Ready Dust in the Brick Furnace for 200 seconds.
- Fuse: Fine Fuse Powder + Rope in the Hardwood Table.
- Black Powder: 5 Potassium Dust + 2 Sulfur Dust + Charcoal → 3; alternatively 3 Gunpowder + Sulfur Dust + 2 Charcoal → 4.
- Weak Dynamite: Fuse on top, Red Dye center, four Black Powder in pairs on both sides, and Copper Dust below.
- Hold Weak Dynamite in the main hand and a Firestarter/Flint and Steel in the offhand; right-click a block. The charge takes 4.5 seconds to explode, deals up to 12 damage, and affects only ordinary Stone, Cobblestone, Andesite, Granite, Diorite and regular surface Coal/Iron/Copper Ore.
- Blasted materials give Basin byproducts with independent chances improved by 50%. Every chance above 100% guarantees one output, then rolls the remainder for another.
- Basin Charcoal rolls 20% Sulfur Dust and 15% Wood Dust; Coal independently rolls 45% Sulfur, 43% + 23% Stone Dust and 2.4% Carbon Dust.
- Fireclay Forge: Carbon Dust → Pure Carbon Dust in 400 seconds of charcoal heat; it continues across two valid charcoal batches.

## Fixes

- Packed Mud's speed bonus no longer crashes Minecraft on startup; Entity movement now checks the block below the player.
- Flint Axe is removed from Log-to-Planks; Wooden and Hardwood Axes remain the eligible tools. To reach the first Wooden Axe without a loop, cutting one Stripped Log with a Bone Knife yields one Wooden Sheet in personal crafting; later Plank cutting produces three Sheets per Plank.
