# Project Flint v0.28.40-a — Early-game correction

## Intent
Correct two v0.28.39-a balance changes that did not match the requested early-game progression while preserving the restored detailed unified advancement guide.

## Player-facing behavior
- Primitive Twig Pile ignition succeeds **65%** of the time.
- Crude Stone Cutters once again yield up to **2 Plant Fiber** from a full four-layer Leaf Litter block; their gathering output is not nerfed.
- Crude Stone Cutters now require **2 Very Refined Stone + 1 Plant Tool Binding + 1 Lightly Burnt Twig**.
- An old in-progress cutter assembly containing the previous second binding refunds that extra binding when completed.

## Architecture
The existing `HarvestingToolAssemblyBlock` keeps its save-compatible `BINDINGS` state property, but newly formed assemblies treat the starter Plant Tool Binding as the complete binding cost. This avoids a save/state migration while enforcing the new recipe.

## Design reasoning
The intended progression improvement is cheaper access to the first dedicated plant cutters, not weaker plant harvesting. Retaining the existing state property also keeps old worlds and in-progress assemblies compatible.

## Tuning snapshot
- Twig Pile ignition: **65%**.
- Full Leaf Litter with Crude Stone Cutters: **2 Plant Fiber**.
- Full Leaf Litter with Crude Multitool: **2 Plant Fiber**.
- Full Leaf Litter with Flint Multitool: **4 Plant Fiber**.
- Full Leaf Litter with Flint Plant Cutters / Advanced Flint Multitool: **6 Plant Fiber**.
- Crude Stone Cutters assembly: **2 Very Refined Stone + 1 Plant Tool Binding + 1 Lightly Burnt Twig**.

## Assets and licenses
No textures, models, sounds, or third-party assets change in this release.

## Advancement coverage
The unified ProjectFlint advancement tree now states the 65% Twig Pile ignition chance, the restored Leaf Litter yields, and the corrected one-binding Crude Stone Cutters assembly.

## Known limitations
No unrelated harvesting, darkness, knapping, or workstation tuning is changed in this correction.

## Validation
The release validator checks the new ignition constant, restored Leaf Litter routing, one-binding assembly behavior, legacy-binding refund, advancement text, version wiring, and cleanup/build protocol.

## Next release pressure
Continue balancing from playtest feedback without reinterpretation of unrelated tool yields or recipe costs.
