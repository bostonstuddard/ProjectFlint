# Project Flint 0.32.1 — Approved Gameplay Fixes

**Base source:** v0.31.69-b reconstructed with sequential approved patches · **Target:** Minecraft 1.21.11 Fabric · **Delivery:** patch-only ZIP.

## Player-facing changes
- Empty Water Pots accept one full Clay Water Bucket, fill to their two-half-bucket capacity, and return the empty Clay Bucket. Partly filled Water Pots reject full-bucket pouring.
- Two-block Sunflowers, Lilacs, Rose Bushes and Peonies harvest once, rather than dropping a duplicate second flower from the upper half.
- Placing Sugar Cane cannot consume a source or flowing water block; planting next to water remains available.
- Advanced Flint ground-tool assemblies show their matching tool-piece textures.
- Placed String (Tripwire) can be recovered without a tool and yields one String.
- Unqualified tools and bare hands cannot finish breaking ores. Crude Hammer Stone processing and legitimate pickaxe/Hammer tier drops are unchanged.
- Removed Clay Grinder wood slab-to-stripped-log reverse conversions; forward recipes remain.
- The Bone Knife + 3 Clay Balls recipe for one Unfired Clay Bucket moved from the Wooden Grinder to the Hardwood Table. The Bone Knife wears once per craft.
- Lumber Processor log chopping takes 90 ticks (4.5 seconds); its washing stage remains 10 seconds.
- Water Bearing Pot now uses 6 Woven Wood + 1 Clay Pot in the Wooden Grinder.
- Standard molded Advanced Kiln cycles use 6 Flint instead of 8. Special kiln cycles are unchanged.
- Sweet Berry and Strawberry Jam now output four per batch.
- Flint Plant Cutters provide five Branches plus existing leaf loot.
- Flint Axe is about a quarter-second faster on logs, but remains slower than Wooden and Hardwood Axes.
- The Wooden Grinder recipe for three Wattled Daub Windows uses 4 Wattled Daub Bricks and 1 Advanced Kiln Glass.
- New Wooden Grinder Wattle route: 3 Sugar Cane + 2 Branches → 4 Wattle.
- Woven Chest Frame: 2 Leather + 4 Woven Wood; advanced Woven Wood processing gains two recipes involving Grinded Slabs and Flint Files.
- Hardwood Table crafts four Wooden Cabinets per existing recipe and three Hardwood from the Wood Dust recipe using a Clay Water Bucket.
- Mutton Pie: 4 Cooked Mutton + 2 Sugar → 3 Mutton Pies. Bookshelf Book: 8 Sugarcane Rolls + 1 Leather → 1 Book.
- The special Project Flint Brush now takes 35 ticks (1.75 seconds) per brush action.
- Mortar Kiln Base: 24 Mortar Bricks instead of 32.
- Eligible adult cows always drop at least one Raw Leather; Black/Brown Bears drop 2–3 Raw Leather alongside existing loot.
