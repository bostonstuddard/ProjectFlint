# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.22.6

## Intent

Apply the new master assets and add the requested woven storage, brush-driven renewable clay/glass, cooked calamari, mortar storage, placed-component, and Sleeping Bag repairs without retuning unrelated progression.

## Player-facing behavior

- Placed Burnt Branches now show one-for-one and two-for-two; placed Flint uses vanilla Flint art.
- The connected two-block Sleeping Bag uses Minecraft's real bed lifecycle for occupancy, sleep animation, wake-up, and night skipping.
- Reusable Sanding Paper files Ink Sacs into Black Dye and Plant Tool Bindings into Woven Fans.
- Physical ground assembly creates a Reed Backpack from 8 fans + 2 bindings or a Bone Brush from 4 fans + 4 Filed Bones.
- Wearing the infinite-durability backpack in the chest slot unlocks all nine hotbar slots and grants 0.5 armor.
- A fan used on Mortar Bricks creates a persistent five-slot Mortar Chest; Flint-tier dismantling recovers it intact.
- Brushing Sand for 3.5 seconds gives one Sand Flake and turns the block into Clay. Four flakes form a Sand Ball; kiln firing creates a Glass Shard.
- Raw Calamari cooks in either heat processor. Bone Club durability is 160 and Mortar Bricks now have a proper item name.

## Architecture

Vanilla `BedBlock` owns sleep lifecycle. `PrimitiveInventorySystem` owns dynamic five/nine-slot capacity. `WovenGearAssemblyBlock` owns physical equipment assembly, `MortarChestBlockEntity` owns five-slot persistence, `BoneBrushItem` owns sustained brushing, and `PrimitiveProcessingRegistry` owns the two new heat recipes.

## Design reasoning

The backpack makes carrying capacity an equipment technology rather than a permanent UI exception. Bed inheritance removes custom sleep bookkeeping that could eject a player after one tick. Renewable Clay and Glass remain multi-step physical operations consistent with the existing no-crafting-table opening.

## Assets and licenses

Every changed or added Project Flint PNG is an exact byte-for-byte copy from the project-owner-supplied master archive or direct attached Firestarter PNG. No texture pixels were edited. Minecraft's built-in Flint and wool textures are referenced by model identifier and are not redistributed.

## Advancement coverage

New milestones cover Black Dye filing, Woven Fan, Reed Backpack, Bone Brush, Mortar Chest, Sand Flake, Sand Ball, Glass Shard, and Cooked Calamari.

## Validation

The release validator checks JSON/resources, exact texture provenance, dynamic carrying capacity, equipment stats, physical formulas, 70-tick brushing, five-slot storage, Flint-tier recovery, BedBlock inheritance, connected models, placed component counts, and both new heat recipes. A local Java 21 build and first-client smoke test remain required if Fabric Loom cannot resolve in the packaging environment.

## Known limitations

- The Mortar Chest deliberately uses Minecraft's compact five-slot hopper-style screen instead of adding a new GUI texture.
- Equipped backpack capacity is functional; a custom third-person armor render is not included.

## Next release pressure

Smoke-test sleep/night skip in singleplayer and multiplayer, both Sleeping Bag orientations and break halves, equip/unequip with full slots, reconnect with stored Mortar Chest contents, every physical assembly count, sustained brush interruption, and Campfire/Kiln outputs.
