# Project Flint v0.28.21-a — Flint Torch Holder

## Intent
Introduce the first long-duration, reusable settlement light that feels materially and structurally earned rather than behaving like a free vanilla torch. The Flint Torch Holder deliberately builds on the existing Wooden Grinder, Super Heated Clay, Mud, Flint, Woven Wood, Wattle-and-Daub shelter geometry, and firestarter progression.

## Player-facing behavior
Three new Wooden Grinder recipes create the fixture in stages:

- 24 Super Heated Clay + 16 Mud Piles → 1 Torch Holder.
- 16 vanilla Flint + 1 Woven Wood → 1 Unlit Flint Torch.
- 1 Torch Holder + 1 Unlit Flint Torch → 1 Flint Torch Holder.

All three outputs stack to one.

The combined Flint Torch Holder is the placeable item. Successful placement creates an **unlit** wall fixture. It can be destroyed with the player's hand or any tool, but the placed fixture has no survival recovery/drop path.

A valid unlit fixture accepts exactly one vanilla Flint as its next fuel charge. Once loaded, it can be ignited with a vanilla Torch, Crude Plant Torch, Flint and Steel, Flint Firestarter, or Bone Firestarter. Flint and Steel / Project Flint firestarters lose one durability when used to ignite the fixture.

A successful ignition produces light level 12 for 40 minutes. At the end of that burn, the loaded Flint charge is spent and the fixture returns to its reusable unlit state. It can then receive another Flint and be ignited again.

## Architecture
`FlintTorchBlock` is the persistent placed fixture and owns orientation, lit state, placement validation, interaction routing, particles, no-loot behavior, and light-state presentation. `FlintTorchBlockEntity` stores the loaded-Flint flag and remaining burn ticks, persists both fields through save/load, and continuously revalidates the installation.

The block uses one registry ID for both lit and unlit states rather than swapping between different blocks. `LIT` therefore drives both the block model and emitted-light function, while the block entity owns fuel state.

The safety validator uses `WattledDaubBricksBlock.verticalStructuralLayer(...)` as the authoritative definition of structural wall height instead of independently guessing Y-levels. A fixture support cell must be a normal Wattled Daub Brick on layer 2 or 3.

## Design reasoning
This fixture is intentionally much more demanding than the five-minute Crude Plant Torch. It is settlement infrastructure, not portable convenience.

The large material cost makes the reusable 40-minute light a meaningful upgrade. The strict Wattle-and-Daub mounting rules also prevent the player from turning the item into a generic cave torch. A developed shelter needs a safe middle-wall mounting position and protected overhead construction before it can support this technology.

The fixture never refunds itself after placement. The manufactured holder is therefore a construction commitment, while the repeat expense is the Flint fuel charge.

## Tuning snapshot
- Torch Holder recipe: 24 Super Heated Clay + 16 Mud Piles → 1.
- Unlit Flint Torch recipe: 16 Flint + 1 Woven Wood → 1.
- Flint Torch Holder recipe: Torch Holder + Unlit Flint Torch → 1.
- Item stack sizes: 1 / 1 / 1.
- Valid support: normal Wattled Daub Brick only.
- Valid structural support layers: 2 and 3 only.
- Same-height safety neighborhood: 3×3×1; only Air, Wattled Daub Bricks, or Wattled Daub Windows may occupy neighboring cells.
- Protected canopy: complete 3×3 Wattled-Daub wall-material plane one or two blocks overhead.
- Fuel per burn: 1 vanilla Flint.
- Burn duration: 48,000 ticks / 40 minutes.
- Emitted light: 12.
- Placed recovery: none.

## Known limitations
- This is a deliberately wall-mounted light only; there is no freestanding or ceiling variant.
- The fixture accepts only one Flint charge at a time; there is no bulk fuel inventory.
- The exact safety envelope is intentionally strict and may receive player-facing visualization/feedback in a later release if placement failures are difficult to understand.
- A full Gradle build may remain unavailable in the sandbox when Fabric Loom 1.14.10 is absent from the local plugin cache; release validation does not substitute for a successful external Gradle build.

## Validation
- Resource JSON files parse successfully.
- Release validation checks the three recipes, stack limits, support-layer restrictions, safe-neighbor/canopy logic, no-loot registration, 40-minute timer, light level 12, block-entity registration, ignition sources, advancements, and exact owner-supplied texture hashes.
- The v0.28.21-a build/cleanup wiring is checked against the project's three-component versioning scheme.

## Next release pressure
