# Project Flint 0.31.31-b

## Intent
Continue the v0.31.30-b Strawberry introduction with dedicated planting seeds and berry foods; add a clay-brick fabrication/recycling loop, repair placed String recovery, and tune Well output efficiency without changing its structure.

## Player-facing behavior
String physically placed as tripwire and items displayed with Place in World can be recovered. Strawberry fruit is edible, while Strawberry Seeds establish new plants and replace fruit in creative-block cloning and cutter-uproot recovery. The crop's existing cutter-only survival harvesting and biome generation are unchanged. Strawberry Jam and Strawberry Pie now use their supplied sprites and the existing research/oven workflow.

The Wooden Grinder can craft an Unfired Brick Mold with 8 Wooden Sheets and a Bone Knife (knife loses durability, as other tool recipes do). Use that reusable mold with 8 vanilla Clay Balls for 8 Unfired Bricks. Unfired Bricks fire into vanilla Bricks and can recycle into 2 Unfired Clay Balls each; 2 finished vanilla Bricks yield 12 Wattled Daub Bricks. Four vanilla Bricks produce one vanilla Bricks block, including via Wooden Grinder. A Bone Knife with 3 Clay Balls also crafts an Unfired Clay Bucket.

## Architecture
Brick-form and alternate bucket recipes live in `WoodenGrinderRecipeRegistry`; brick recycling also has a Clay Grinder recipe/guide and optional shapeless recipes. Furnace-family recipes are data JSON, and heat-station rules live in `PrimitiveProcessingRegistry`; the existing parchment system still owns researched berry foods, with matching `BookshelfRecipeRegistry` research. String's loot exemption applies only to placed tripwire, not broad monster/world loot. PF-EMI presentation remains wholly external.

## Design reasoning
The Unfired Brick Mold is distinct from the existing clay weapon mold and is retained through brick batches. Strawberry seeds separate propagation from direct food usage. Superlinear rope efficiency rewards concentrating 16 ropes in one well rather than deploying one in each of multiple wells.

## Tuning snapshot
- Strawberry nutrition: 1 (the minimum positive hunger value); saturation modifier: 0.05, down from 0.15. Strawberry plants still yield 1–2 fruit.
- Strawberry Jam: 12 Strawberries + 1 Sugar + 2 Wooden Cups -> 2 Strawberry Jam. Sweet Berry Jam remains 8 Sweet Berries.
- Strawberry Pie: 2 Strawberry Jam + 2 Strawberries + 1 Sugar + 1 Butter -> 2 Strawberry Pies.
- Brick form: 8 Wooden Sheets + Bone Knife -> 1 reusable Unfired Brick Mold; mold + 8 Clay Balls -> 8 Unfired Bricks; each unfired brick -> 1 vanilla Brick, or 2 Unfired Clay Balls; 2 vanilla Bricks -> 12 Wattled Daub Bricks; 4 vanilla Bricks -> 1 Bricks block.
- Bucket: 3 Clay Balls + Bone Knife -> Unfired Clay Bucket (former Woven Chest Frame option retained); fire in any normal heat station.
- Aquifer base: Scarce 13%, Normal 68%, Good 94%, Abundant 130%; 0–16 ropes add `3*n + floor(n²/4)` percentage points, yielding +3 at 1, +40 at 8, +112 at 16.
- Active Well: low-volume minecart riding sound at 70-tick intervals, no sound when invalid or waiting on a full output buffer.

Their targets and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the texture-provenance documents. Prior asset provenance remains unchanged.

## Advancement coverage
Minecraft/ProjectFlint advancements remain disabled. New Strawberry food research uses existing Bookshelf-bound Recipe Parchments; Flint Quests integration is unmodified.

## Known limitations
Existing worlds will retain their current Strawberry Plants, but fruit can no longer be directly replanted: gather or obtain new Strawberry Seeds. Normal vanilla furnace/campfire cooking is registered through datapack recipes; custom heat stations use their authoritative processing registry. Vanilla hunger values are integer units, so 1 nutrition cannot be lowered to another positive value without a separate fractional-hunger system; this release reduces saturation instead.

## Validation
Validator covers recipes, registrations, asset hashes, v31 cleanup, well scaling and sound, string recovery, and unchanged external PF-EMI boundary. Full Gradle compilation depends on resolving Fabric Loom 1.14.10 and is not asserted without actually executing it.
