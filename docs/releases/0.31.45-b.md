# Project Flint v0.31.45-b

## Intent
Close unintended primitive mining bypasses, finish the Fat Torch and kiln visuals, repair fish damage/tooltip behavior, and convert staged Hardwood art into an actual resource-to-workstation progression.

## Player-facing behavior
- Hand, food items, and unrelated tools do not mine restricted dirt, rock or ore. The server rejects forbidden destruction even when an incorrect client sends completion. Hand Grass scraping remains legal.
- Bushes cannot be cleared by unarmored bare hands. Refined Rock/appropriate cutters grant Twigs normally; a Tanned Utility Vest may clear vegetation into a vanilla Stick.
- Wooden and Hardwood Pickaxes harvest regular Coal Ore into Coal (1 per block). Coal has no Flint torch or Forge fuel role yet.
- Fish can now be damaged/killed; buckets of fish do not display Hunger/Saturation.
- Fat-Burning Torches can be placed on supported walls/floors. The Fat ingredient provides one starting charge, and one additional Fat can be inserted per relight. Firestarter ignition consumes one charge, then burns for 30 minutes (sheltered against rain).
- Finished Clay Kiln bottom/top render with Brick Shingles instead of raw clay.
- Basin accepts any plank and outputs 1 Wood Dust for 1 hunger point, like the original Tannin process. Planks remain separately recoverable until processed.
- Two planks in a vertical crafting recipe produce 2 Sticks.
- Craft **Hardwood**: 1 Clay Water Bucket, 2 Wood Dust, 1 Tannin; returns an empty Clay Bucket. Smelt/forge **Hardwood Alloy** from 1 Hardwood during one uninterrupted full **five-minute Charcoal** Fireclay Forge burn.
- Craft **Hardwood Crafting Table**: 2 Hardwood Alloy + 1 Cobblestone + 1 Lumber Processor. Place/relocate like a normal recoverable workstation using wood axes or Flint Axe.
- Table has a 3×3 crafting grid. Every output must wait 60 ticks (3 seconds) after putting in materials, including subsequent shift-crafts.
- Hardwood Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Sword: vanilla 3×3 tool shapes with Hardwood in the head/blade positions and Sticks in the handle. Ordinary wooden tools can also be made at the same grid using Planks + Sticks.
- Hardwood Hammer: rows [Alloy, Alloy, Alloy], [Alloy, Alloy, Alloy], [Leather, Stick, Rope]. The stone-tier head mines vanilla stone-tier/lower ores and deals heavy melee damage to fish and other mobs.

## Architecture
The mining rate is constrained through `BlockStateBaseMixin`, first-attack and `BEFORE` break permission checks, and an independent authoritative `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin` guard. Existing special drop handlers are preserved. Torch supports facing-based wall states with persisted fat charges on its BlockEntity. Forge alloy timer and output reuse existing separate input, fuel, igniter and primary output slots. Hardwood workstation subclasses vanilla crafting but swaps the output slot for a 60-tick server pickup delay.

## Design reasoning
A tool tier must govern both completion and drops, not just cosmetic mining progress. Fabric's existing server-side gate prevents malicious/out-of-sync vanilla break completion. The Forge must consume Charcoal (not generic fuel) to produce Hardwood Alloy. Reusing the existing Lumber Processor visual texture palette retains approved hand-authored artwork without AI generation.

## Tuning snapshot
- Wood Dust: 1 Plank per landing → 1 dust, hunger -1 point.
- Sticks: 2 Planks → 2 Sticks.
- Hardwood: 2 dust + 1 Tannin + 1 full Clay Water Bucket → 1 Hardwood + 1 empty Clay Bucket.
- Alloy: 1 Hardwood and a complete 6,000-tick Charcoal Forge burn → 1 Alloy.
- Table: 2 Alloy + 1 Cobblestone + 1 Lumber Processor, 60-tick output wait.
- Hardwood tools: 92 durability, 2.5 mining speed versus vanilla wood's 59 durability/2.0 speed.
- Hammer: 196 durability, Stone mining tier, 4.0 base mining speed, strong attack.
- Fat torch: 30-minute burn, one starting Fat charge from the recipe, 1 Fat per further charge, maximum 8 charges stored, stays lit under rain shelter.

## Assets and licenses
No AI-authored textures introduced. Existing owner-approved Lumber Processor textures were darkened for the new Hardwood Crafting Table's side/top/bottom. Existing Brick Shingles texture was assigned to the finished Kiln body/top. All other staged Hardwood item textures are retained unchanged.

## Advancement coverage
Flint Quests is authoritative for progression; Minecraft advancement files remain retired per current cleanup lineage. New progression targets for the external quest content: Plank → Wood Dust, Hardwood, first Charcoal Alloy burn, Hardwood Crafting Table, first delayed craft, Hardwood Hammer, first Coal Ore harvest. No hard dependency on Flint Quests was added.

## Known limitations
This source patch is untested in a running Minecraft client. The 3-second delay is enforced server-side even though the vanilla crafting result will appear immediately in the client slot. The Client GUI does not yet show a numeric countdown. The Forge GUI likewise lacks a dedicated visible Hardwood Alloy progress bar. Coal is deliberately an intermediate dormant resource.
