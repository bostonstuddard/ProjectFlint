# Project Flint v0.28.7.4-a — Forage, Sweet Berry, and Bear Model Correction

## Intent
Correct the remaining primitive forage inconsistencies, make Loose Soil propagation self-consistent, make Sweet Berry progression tool-gated instead of vanilla-breakable, make bears somewhat encounterable, and finally render each bear against the model/UV layout its texture was authored for.

## Player-facing behavior
- Black Bear spawn weight: 6; Brown Bear spawn weight: 4; both remain 1–2 per natural spawn group.
- Digging Dirt, Grass Block, or Loose Soil with a Multitool gives each adjacent Dirt/Grass Block a 33% chance to become Loose Soil.
- Project Flint forage bushes never drop themselves through survival tool harvesting; they drop twigs only. Better cutter tiers yield more twigs.
- Leaf Litter drops scale by its 1–4 segment amount at 25/50/75/100 percent of the previous full-pile yield, rounded up.
- Sweet Berry Bushes require a plant-cutter-capable tool for berry picking. Picking gives 1–2 berries and preserves the bush.
- Flint Plant Cutters tier can spend 6.5 seconds removing the bush for exactly one Sweet Berry Seed; Advanced Flint Multitool is treated as the later equivalent.
- Sweet Berry Seeds establish a new Sweet Berry Bush when used on top of Grass Block.
- Sweet Berries are reduced to 0.5 hunger / 0.3 saturation.
- Sweet Berry Jam and Sweet Berry Pie exist as asset-backed future items only; no recipes/eating behavior were invented.

## Architecture
- `ToolCapabilities.canDirectHarvestPlant` explicitly excludes Project Flint forage bushes so their custom drop pipeline cannot be bypassed by stronger Multitools.
- `ModItems.isPlantCutterLike` centralizes plant-cutter qualification without broadening unrelated cutter capabilities.
- Sweet Berry right-click harvesting is owned by `PrimitiveInteractionSystem`; seed extraction is completed in `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin`, with destroy time gated in `BlockStateBaseMixin`.
- Bear rendering uses separate `BlackBearModel` and `BrownBearModel` layer definitions registered through Fabric's model-layer registry.

## Design reasoning
A more advanced tool should improve forage yield, not convert a progression bush into a collectible block. Loose Soil should propagate by the same rule that creates it so the mechanic is learnable. Sweet Berries remain renewable early food, while moving/replanting the bush becomes a later Flint Plant Cutter capability. The bear texture issue was structural: the source textures were authored for different model geometry/UVs, so sharing a Polar Bear layer could not be corrected reliably with a small UV tweak.

## Tuning snapshot
- Black Bear weight: 6.
- Brown Bear weight: 4.
- Loose Soil adjacent conversion chance: 33% per adjacent Dirt/Grass Block.
- Bush twigs: Refined Rock 3–4; crude cutter tier 4; Flint tier 6; Advanced Flint Multitool 8.
- Leaf Litter fraction: 1=25%, 2=50%, 3=75%, 4=100%, ceiling-rounded.
- Sweet Berry harvest: 1–2 berries.
- Sweet Berry Bush seed break: 130 ticks (6.5 s), one seed.
- Sweet Berry food: nutrition 1 (0.5 hunger icon), saturation modifier 0.15 (0.3 actual saturation).

## Advancement coverage
No new recipe/knowledge advancement was added. Sweet Berry Jam/Pie are intentionally future-content stubs; the seed mechanic is currently a direct survival interaction rather than a new progression gate.

## Known limitations
- Jam and Pie are registered/displayable only; their eventual production and food values remain intentionally undefined.
- Bear spawn frequency still participates in Minecraft's normal passive-creature spawn competition/cap, so observed encounter rate depends on biome and existing passive populations.

## Validation
- Resource JSON parses are checked by `tools/validate_release.py`.
- Validator checks the 6/4 bear weights, separate 128×64 model definitions, 33% Loose Soil propagation, forage-bush direct-harvest exclusion, leaf-litter segment scaling, Sweet Berry seed/harvest gates, exact user-asset hashes, and build/cleanup versioning.
- The external bear model source was compared against the original Bear Minimum model classes; Black and Brown use distinct geometry and UV offsets.

## Next release pressure
Playtest actual bear encounter frequency, Sweet Berry renewable-food pacing, and the 33% Loose Soil cascade before changing those numbers again. Decide Jam/Pie crafting only when their primitive preservation/cooking process is designed.
