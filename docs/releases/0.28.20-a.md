# Project Flint v0.28.20-a

## Intent
Refine Wattled Daub into a more usable shelter system without weakening its primitive structural limits, fix Flint Plant Cutter Sugar Cane harvesting, and let the Wooden Grinder meaningfully accelerate Wattle/Daub production once the player has earned that infrastructure.

## Player-facing behavior
- Wattled Daub roof pieces may now extend up to 3 blocks horizontally from a valid four-high load-bearing column instead of 2.
- Structural disconnection has a short grace period before unsupported pieces fall, giving the player time to swap a wall brick for a Window without instantly losing the roof above it.
- Wattled Daub Windows must sit directly on a Wattled Daub Brick and may only occupy wall layer 2 or 3 of the four-block wall height.
- Windows cannot occupy layer 1 or layer 4 and cannot stack directly on another Window.
- Adjacent Wattled Daub Windows hide their touching internal cube faces, eliminating the visible inside faces seen through side-by-side transparent windows.
- The Wattled Daub Door now uses the newly supplied dedicated inventory sprite; its placed door art is unchanged.
- Flint Plant Cutters and the Advanced Flint Multitool harvest the whole Sugar Cane column from the clicked block upward and produce one Sugarcane Roll per cane segment.
- Clay and Wooden Grinders can convert 4 Flint Chips into 1 Flint and split 1 Flint back into 4 Flint Chips.
- Wooden Grinder: 1 Sugarcane Roll + 1 Plant Tool Binding -> 2 Woven Fans.
- Wooden Grinder: 1 Woven Fan + 1 Wattle -> 3 Tied Wattle.
- Wooden Grinder: 1 Flint Chip + 1 Mud Pile -> 3 Daub Piles.
- Wooden Grinder: 4 Tied Wattle + 2 Wet Daub Piles -> 4 Unfired Wattle and Daub.

## Architecture
`WattledDaubBricksBlock` remains the structural authority. The four-cell vertical limit is unchanged, while `MAX_CEILING_REACH` is raised to 3. Structural validation now exposes a 1-based vertical layer helper so specialized structural pieces can enforce their own valid positions without duplicating foundation logic.

`WattledDaubWindowBlock` narrows that general structural rule: the immediate block below must be an ordinary Wattled Daub Brick and the resulting vertical layer must be 2 or 3. Its same-block `skipRendering` override removes mutual interior faces between adjacent windows without modifying texture pixels.

The structural recheck uses a delayed scheduled tick. This preserves gravity/crumble behavior while creating an actual renovation window between breaking a wall brick and placing its replacement.

Flint Plant Cutter Sugar Cane processing is handled before the generic cutter path in `ServerPlayerGameModeMixin`, ensuring the full cane column is counted and transformed instead of falling through to vanilla's single selected-block drop.

## Design reasoning
Primitive walls should be restrictive, but renovating them should not be mechanically hostile. Three-block roof reach makes small rooms practical while retaining a clear need for four-high supporting columns. Restricting Windows to the middle two wall layers makes them actual openings rather than alternate foundations or roof pieces.

The Wooden Grinder recipes intentionally improve throughput for processes the player has already discovered by hand. It is an efficiency technology, not a progression bypass.

## Tuning snapshot
- Maximum Wattled Daub structural height: 4 cells.
- Roof reach from a valid four-high column: 3 blocks horizontally.
- Window legal layers: 2 and 3 only.
- Window immediate sill: Wattled Daub Brick only.
- Structural replacement grace: 10 ticks (0.5 seconds).
- Flint Plant Cutter Sugar Cane yield: 1 Sugarcane Roll per removed cane block.
- Flint sizing: 4 Flint Chips <-> 1 Flint.
- Wooden Grinder Woven Fan: 1 Sugarcane Roll + 1 Plant Tool Binding -> 2.
- Wooden Grinder Tied Wattle: 1 Woven Fan + 1 Wattle -> 3.
- Wooden Grinder Daub: 1 Flint Chip + 1 Mud Pile -> 3.
- Wooden Grinder Unfired Wattle/Daub: 4 Tied Wattle + 2 Wet Daub Piles -> 4.

## Assets and licenses
`assets/projectflint/textures/item/wattled_daub_door.png` is the direct owner-provided `watled daub door item texture.png`, copied byte-for-byte. Existing Wattled Daub Window and placed Door textures are unchanged. No texture pixels were generated, resized, recolored, composited, cropped, or otherwise edited.

## Advancement coverage
- Existing Wattle, Tied Wattle, Daub, Window, and Wattled Daub progression advancements continue to apply to the new production routes because the outputs are the same registered items.
- Existing Sugarcane Roll inventory advancement is awarded by the corrected Flint Plant Cutter column harvest.
- No new item-only advancement is required for the Door sprite replacement.

## Known limitations
- Window internal faces connect natively, but this release does not fabricate a new border-level CTM texture sheet from the owner's single 16x16 Window asset. A dedicated connected-window art set can be added later if desired.
- Wattled Daub Door remains creative-only until a fitting woodworking/joinery technology exists.
- Full Gradle assembly may still depend on Fabric Loom availability in the local build environment.

## Validation
- All JSON resources parse during release validation.
- Validator checks the 3-block roof reach, delayed structural recheck, Window layer/sill restrictions, adjacent Window face culling, full-column Sugar Cane processing, Flint conversion recipes, Wooden Grinder dual recipes, and the Wattled Daub Door item texture SHA-256.
- The existing unsupported-fall drop guard remains validated so falling Wattled Daub cannot duplicate intact + crumble drops.

## Next release pressure

- Develop the woodworking/joinery technology that makes the Wattled Daub Door obtainable in survival.
- Continue turning the Wooden Grinder into a production-efficiency tier rather than a universal recipe bypass.
