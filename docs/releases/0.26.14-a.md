# Project Flint v0.26.14-a

## Clay Grinder UV fix

The Clay Grinder keeps the custom `clay_grinder_wheel.png` texture. Its painted 8x12 region is now explicitly mapped to every face of the center grinder body. On the 12x12 east/west faces Minecraft stretches that painted region to fit the full face, rather than displaying transparent portions of the 16x16 source texture.

No texture PNGs were modified.
