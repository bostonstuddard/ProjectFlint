# Project Flint v0.31.51-b — Hardwood crafting and ore progression

## Intent
Expand Hardwood Crafting Table progression and close unintended ore harvest paths.

## Player-facing behavior
- Hardwood Tools now have **320 durability** rather than 92; Hardwood Hammer has **560** rather than 196. Axe, Pickaxe, Hoe, Sword and Hammer require Leather Straps; Shovel remains strap-free.
- Two planks produce **three regular Sticks** in the Hardwood Crafting Table. Two Hardwood produce **two Hardwood Sticks**. Hardwood Sticks are a distinct registered item using existing Minecraft art until dedicated human-sourced art is provided.
- Natural Stone and Coal Ore can be harvested with Wooden Pickaxe, Hardwood Pickaxe or Hardwood Hammer, all at **13.5 seconds** per block (270 ticks). Hardwood Hammer produces **two Coal** from one Coal Ore, while a pickaxe yields one.
- Ordinary Iron Ore and Copper Ore require Hardwood Hammer or an appropriately tiered vanilla pickaxe. Deepslate Iron/Copper, and higher-tier ores, do not gain a Hardwood-tier harvest path.
- Unauthorized tools, hands and food can destroy ore only through **very slow** mining (45 seconds at 20 TPS), without drops. Creative breaking is unchanged.
- Added Hardwood Table-only crafting for Bone Knife/Filed Bone/Flint File, Wood Firestarter, Bookshelf, Recipe Parchment, Wood Sheets/Boards/Screws, Brick Molds/Unfired Bricks/Fireclay, brick/slab/stair variants, Brick Furnace, paper/books/dyes, Bone/Leather/Tanned armor, wooden slabs (2 planks → 6 slabs), beams, cabinets, chests, nest, Well Pulley, door variants and staged Rope.
- Rope Stage 2 remains deliberately skipped: Stage 1 combines with four screws and four sheets to create Stage 3, then is finished with a Flint File.
- New Hardwood crafting recipes use a **separate recipe type** to keep them out of the default vanilla crafting category, but remain compatible with the timed 60-tick Hardwood Table.
- Older duplicate vanilla crafting paths for wooden axes/pickaxes/shovels and hinged doors no longer load; old unfired-brick/clay-bucket smelting, smoking, blasting and campfire recipes are excluded from regular vanilla furnace categories. Normal food-smelting recipes remain.

## Architecture
Hardwood recipes use their own registered recipe type and serializers, while keeping vanilla shaped/shapeless/durable matching. Mining uses one permission table shared by client progression and server destruction.

## Design reasoning
Tool and drop permissions must agree: a player may eventually remove an unusable ore at hand speed, but cannot gain ore drops ahead of their unlocked mining tier.

## Crafting details / remainder handling
- Flint File and Bone Knife are durability-based reusable tools in workstation recipes; the Unfired Brick Mold is returned unchanged.
- The Clay Water Bucket is consumed as an ingredient but yields the empty Clay Bucket; only its contained water is spent.
- Leather, bone and tanned armor use normal vanilla armor silhouettes with their specified materials.
- Dye crafting outputs are triple the standard vanilla recipe outputs; cactus and sea pickle smelting overrides also yield three dyes.

## Tuning snapshot
- Wooden-tier natural Stone/Coal and Hammer Iron/Copper: 270 ticks (13.5 seconds).
- Unauthorized ore breaking: 900 ticks (45 seconds), no drops.
- Hardwood tools: 320 durability; Hammer: 560.
- Hardwood crafting: 60 server ticks per result.

## Assets and licenses
No new third-party assets; the Hardwood Sticks model reuses the vanilla Stick artwork.

## Known limitations
- No AI-made texture assets: the Hardwood Sticks item temporarily references the existing vanilla Stick sprite.
- The dedicated Project Flint Hardwood crafting recipe type may need support in PF-EMI's recipe viewer before recipes appear in a new dedicated viewer category. The 3x3 table itself reads and processes those recipes.
- There is no Gradle wrapper or Gradle CLI available in this sandbox, so a full mod build or live Minecraft client test could not be run. The source and recipe format were statically checked instead.

## Validation
Static recipe/registry checks and preservation validation performed. No complete Gradle build or client runtime tests performed.

## Test matrix
1. Confirm 60-tick delay, recipe output, tool/bucket remainders, shift-click and rerun cooldown for at least one shaped, shapeless and durable Hardwood recipe.
2. Try breaking every stone ore with an empty hand, bread, Woodland Axes, and the different pickaxe tiers; verify time and drops are correct in survival and that creative remains free.
3. Test Coal Ore with Wooden/Hardwood Pickaxe versus Hammer; Hammer must grant exactly two Coal, otherwise one.
4. Test normal Iron/Copper Ore with Hammer, stone/wood pickaxes; deepslate versions must not grant Hammer drops.
5. Craft tools, hammer, Bone Knife/File, all book/paper recipes, cabinets/chests, all doors, each Rope stage, and the two-sheet/board/screw conversion paths.
6. Ensure unused legacy variants no longer appear in ordinary crafting/smelting categories and the new Hardwood recipes do not leak into the vanilla category.
