# Project Flint v0.31.9-b — Milking, Grinder Ambiguity, and Bookshelf Recovery Hotfix

## Intent
Repair three gameplay regressions found immediately after the v0.31.7-b/v0.31.8-b progression additions without changing the intended recipes, costs, or progression gates.

## Player-facing behavior
A single Wooden Cup now correctly becomes a Wooden Cup with Raw Milk when a cow is milked. Wooden Grinder dual-input recipes that share ingredients but use different counts can now coexist in the recipe-preview row; for example, excess Wooden Sheets/Screws no longer hide the Wooden Chest Frame behind the Bookshelf recipe. Bookshelves broken and replaced retain their installed Book count.

## Architecture
Wooden Cup filling now delegates stack exchange to vanilla `ItemUtils.createFilledResult`. `WoodenGrinderRecipeRegistry` exposes all matching dual-input definitions and `WoodenGrinderMenu` maps those matches into its existing six preview/result selections. `BookshelfItem` restores its saved custom-data count at `BlockPlaceContext#getClickedPos()` directly instead of applying a second face offset.

## Design reasoning
The milk fix follows Minecraft's standard filled-container semantics and handles one-item stacks, larger stacks, inventory insertion, dropping, and creative mode consistently. Ambiguous grinder recipes are a selection problem rather than an ordering problem, so exposing all matches avoids merely swapping which recipe becomes inaccessible. Bookshelf placement uses the placement context's already-resolved target block, preventing saved state from being written one block past the workstation.

## Tuning snapshot
No recipe counts, food values, durability values, mining speeds, world-generation rates, workstation speeds, or stack sizes changed in v0.31.9-b.

## Assets and licenses
No textures, models, sounds, or externally sourced assets were added or modified.

## Advancement coverage
No advancement criteria or progression descriptions changed. Existing Wooden Cup, Wooden Chest Frame, Bookshelf, and research advancement coverage is preserved.

## Known limitations
The Wooden Grinder exposes at most six simultaneous recipe previews, matching the workstation's existing UI capacity. Current registered dual-input combinations fit within that limit.

## Validation
Release validation checks the vanilla filled-container milking path, multi-match Wooden Grinder plumbing, direct Bookshelf placement-position restore, version/build wiring, and all inherited Project Flint regression checks. Resource JSON validation remains enabled.

## Next release pressure
Continue runtime testing of overlapping Wooden Grinder recipes and preserved workstation state, especially after repeated crafting/break/place cycles and with full inventories.
