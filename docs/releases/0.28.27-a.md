# Project Flint v0.28.27-a

## Intent
Replace the temporary physical-block aquifer prototype with the intended invisible groundwater system and give the creative locator its finished Dowsing Rod artwork.

## Virtual aquifers
- Aquifers no longer generate water blocks, clay markers, features, entities, or persistent world data.
- Groundwater is derived on demand from the **world seed + logical X/Z cell coordinates**, similar in spirit to how an invisible chunk property can exist without a physical marker.
- The field uses 32×32 logical cells with approximately a 1-in-7 aquifer rate.
- Each aquifer represents a virtual five-column cross footprint.
- Its representative groundwater depth is deterministically placed about 20–26 blocks below the local world-generation surface.
- Aquifer location/existence is seed-based; biome humidity changes the future water **yield** rather than changing the location algorithm.

## Old-world / forward-port behavior
There is nothing to generate and nothing to migrate. An older Project Flint world immediately receives virtual aquifers when queried because the result comes from its existing world seed and coordinates. Newly generated chunks and years-old chunks use the same rule. This also removes the v0.28.26-a dependency on custom configured/placed worldgen features.

The old physical `AquiferFeature`, `ModFeatures` aquifer registry, and aquifer worldgen JSONs are retired by the cleanup BAT. Physical water/clay signatures already created by the short-lived v0.28.26-a prototype are simply ignored by the new system; the patch does not destructively scan old worlds trying to remove water.

## Aquifer Dowsing Rod
- Keeps the existing `projectflint:aquifer_finder` registry ID for world/item compatibility.
- Display name is now **Aquifer Dowsing Rod**.
- Uses the user-supplied 16×16 Dowsing Rod texture byte-for-byte.
- Remains Creative-only.
- Right-click evaluates nearby virtual cells and marks the five surface columns of up to eight nearby aquifers.
- Reports the nearest aquifer distance and biome-derived yield category.

## Future Well contract
`AquiferSystem.findBelow(...)` remains the Well-facing API. With zero horizontal tolerance, a Well must sit over one of the aquifer's five virtual columns and above its representative groundwater depth. Future Well tiers can choose a larger tolerance if their construction supports it.
