# Project Flint 0.31.32-b

## Intent
Extend food, farming, dairy, fireclay masonry, and Brick Furnace progression with explicit GUI affordances, fix abandoned Zombie Horses on the surface, and add rare nighttime wolf encounters. Preserve external PF-EMI ownership.

## Player-facing behavior
A full Clay Water Bucket in the Wooden Grinder hydrates nine Flax Seeds into three vanilla Wheat Seeds, nine Wheat into nine Flour, or one raw meat into one Fat. The Clay Bucket is emptied, not consumed. Milk adult cows directly with an empty Clay Bucket; one Clay Milk Bucket and two empty Wooden Cups give four Milk Cups and the empty Clay Bucket back.

A new parchment research costs eight Fat; its Primitive Oven recipe converts four raw meats into five Fat without water. Bread research uses eight Flour, then three Flour plus two Milk Cups makes two Bread. Sushi research uses eight Raw Calamari, then two Raw Calamari plus two Flour makes two Sushi Rolls. Cheese research uses four Milk Cups, then two Milk Cups and one Fat makes one Cheese. Turtles drop one low-nutrition raw meat, cooked at primitive heat stations and vanilla furnace-family recipes.

Two Unfired Bricks with one Sand Flake yield two Unfired Fireclay Bricks in the Wooden Grinder; heat firing makes Fireclay Bricks. Four fired bricks make a Fireclay Brick Block; Clay Grinder options include slabs, stairs, and recovery of two slabs into one block. Primitive Oven plus eight blocks forms a Brick Furnace. Ordinary Brick Furnace exclusively converts vanilla Leather into Tanned Leather. Fuel and Well rope slots now have a compact ghost marker and hover explanations. Surface Zombie Horses are discarded. Beginning on the sixth night, a 5% independent per-player nightly roll spawns a 3–5 wolf pack at 30–45 block distance where viable surface positions exist.

## Architecture
Recipe logic stays in `WoodenGrinderRecipeRegistry`, `BookshelfRecipeRegistry`, `PrimitiveStoveRecipeRegistry`, `PrimitiveProcessingRegistry`, clay-grinding JSON/guide, and the Brick Furnace-exclusive registry; PF-EMI is external and contains recipe presentation. Filled Clay Bucket return occurs atomically when the grinder consumes a hydration recipe. World wolf rolls are per-player/per-day and cleared with the server lifecycle. Zombie Horses are explicitly handled before the monster-type restriction.

## Design reasoning
Vanilla Wheat Seeds are used for crop compatibility. Hydration consumes only fluid, not ceramics. Bulk Fat production rewards the parchment research. Sushi/cheese are researched cooking steps. Tanned Leather is deliberately restricted to the normal Brick Furnace to give it a dedicated recipe rather than silently changing every kiln. Night packs are limited to one roll per player per night and skipped if no viable surface spot exists.

## Tuning snapshot
- Wheat Seeds: 9 Flax Seeds + Clay Water Bucket -> 3; Flour: 9 Wheat + Clay Water Bucket -> 9; Fat baseline: 1 raw meat + Clay Water Bucket -> 1.
- Fat Recipe: 8 Fat + blank parchment -> bound Fat Recipe; 4 raw meats -> 5 Fat.
- Bread: 3 Flour + 2 Milk Cups -> 2 Bread; Sushi Roll: 2 Raw Calamari + 2 Flour -> 2 rolls; Cheese: 2 Milk Cups + 1 Fat -> 1 Cheese.
- Clay milk: two empty Wooden Cups + Clay Milk Bucket -> four Milk Cups; source bucket returns empty.
- Fireclay: 2 Unfired Bricks + 1 Sand Flake -> 2 Unfired Fireclay Bricks; firing -> one Fireclay Brick each; four bricks -> one block; one block -> two slabs or one stair.
- Night wolves: one 5% roll per surviving non-Creative, non-Spectator Overworld player per night, from night six; pack size 3–5, 30–45 blocks away.

## Assets and licenses
Nine directly supplied sprites remain byte-identical, including the Fireclay Brick Block texture. `clay_milk_bucket.png` recolors the water pixels of ProjectFlint's existing Clay Water Bucket; `tanned_leather.png` darkens Minecraft 1.21.11's vanilla Leather pixel silhouette. Their SHA-256 and origins are in `docs/TEXTURE_PROVENANCE.md` and `docs/texture_provenance.json`. No AI textures introduced.

## Advancement coverage
Minecraft/ProjectFlint advancements remain retired; new food recipes integrate the existing Bookshelf-bound Recipe Parchment system. Flint Quests bridge unchanged.

## Known limitations
The nighttime pack's 5% is the trigger roll, not guaranteed spawning: an unloaded/obstructed surface or absent suitable position can suppress it. Full Gradle compilation requires Fabric Loom 1.14.10, which may be unavailable offline. The clay-grinding guide is an in-mod reference for actual JSON recipes; EMI recipe display remains PF-EMI-owned.

## Validation
Validator checks registrations, recipe counts, milk bucket returns, firing and models, all supplied asset hashes, source cleanup, external EMI dependency, and no prior recipe/darkness regression. In-game runtime testing is still needed.
