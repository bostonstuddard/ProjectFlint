# Project Flint v0.31.38-b — Leather and wooden tool progression

## Intent
Extend post-Utility-Vest personal crafting into early wood tools, leather utility goods and persistent lighting.

## Player-facing behavior
- Backpacks and Utility Vest use short readable slot-count tooltips; tanned armor icons use matching dark leather silhouettes, with the Vest retaining a small gold patch.
- Tanned Leather can be cut with a Bone Knife into Leather Sheet, then two Leather Straps. Repair Kits restore 32 durability to leather/tanned armor. Tool Belt (256 durability) is worn in leggings slot, reduces armor by one and adds ~10% mining speed.
- Slow Wooden Pickaxe breaks Stone into Cobblestone and Crushed Stone into Very Refined Stone, occasionally loosening neighboring Stone. Wooden Axe directly obtains logs; Wooden Shovel harvests earth/sand/clay without triggering soil loosening.
- Personal crafting adds material conversions, log/plank/sheet/board processing with reusable worn tools, wood doors with Wooden Door Hinges, the Basin, and recyclable soil.
- Basin accepts stripped Oak or Birch wood/log, producing two Tannin. Fat-Burning Torch is free-standing, fueled by Charcoal for 30 minutes per charge and extinguishes in exposed rain.
- Brick Furnace alone converts Sand into Glass (no vanilla furnace datapack recipe).
- All new crafting entries are in the new Personal Crafting guide section; Basin has its own page.

## Architecture
ProjectFlint gameplay remains authoritative. Personal Crafting is implemented by ProjectFlint recipe JSON and two serializers for tool durability and armor repair. PF-EMI is external and owns recipe presentation.

## Design reasoning
All tool-assisted conversions wear the tool rather than consuming it. Existing Wooden Screw acts as the bolt in the Tool Belt recipe. Supplied hand-made item textures were imported without replacing unrelated art.

## Tuning snapshot
Wooden Pickaxe ~13.5 seconds per Stone; 15% chance per adjacent Stone to crack; Tool Belt +10% break speed, -1 armor, 256 durability; leather repair +32 durability; 30 minutes/Charcoal torch burn; 1 log + Wooden Axe → 2 planks; 1 plank + Bone Knife → 3 Wooden Sheets.

## Assets and licenses
Five owner-supplied sprites: Tool Belt, Leather Sheet, Leather Strap, Repair Kit and Tannin. Dark leather item textures are recolored from Mojang-authored original leather armor sprites; the Utility Vest has a gold patch. Fat torch sprite is a shaded derivation of vanilla Torch art, no AI imagery.

## Advancement coverage
Vanilla/ProjectFlint advancements remain disabled; future Flint Quests can hook into these gameplay mechanics.

## Known limitations
Full Gradle/JAR/in-game verification requires the user's working Fabric Loom cache; the current isolated environment does not resolve Loom 1.14.10. The Basin is the only functional new prototype station; Drying Rack/Millstone remain uncraftable.

## Validation
JSON syntax, asset references, recipe guide coverage, source-diff audit and simulated patch application are checked. Full Java compile status must be reported separately.

## Next release pressure
In-game crafting book, new-tool mining drops, texture appearance, torch weather/fuel behavior, and interactions with vest-granted inventory should be exercised against live worlds.
