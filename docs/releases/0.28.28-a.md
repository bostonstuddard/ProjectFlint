# Project Flint v0.28.28-a

## Intent
Make ordinary Minecraft water finite so renewable water becomes a technological achievement rather than a two-block trick.

## Player-facing behavior
- Water source blocks no longer regenerate from neighboring source blocks.
- Rivers, lakes, oceans, waterlogged blocks, and player-placed water remain normal water and are not deleted or replaced.
- A reservoir can therefore be drained if the player repeatedly removes its source blocks.
- Older saves receive the rule immediately the next time the server/world starts.

## Architecture
`FiniteWaterSystem` disables Minecraft 1.21.11's native `GameRules.WATER_SOURCE_CONVERSION` rule on server start. No custom fluid, worldgen replacement, chunk migration, or persistent water database is introduced.

## Design reasoning
Surface water should be a finite environmental resource. Renewable production belongs to infrastructure: the future Well will query the seed-deterministic virtual aquifer field from v0.28.27-a rather than relying on vanilla source regeneration.

## Tuning snapshot
- Vanilla water-source conversion: disabled.
- Existing water source blocks: preserved.
- Lava source conversion: unchanged.
- Aquifer generation/storage: none; remains virtual and deterministic.

## Assets and licenses
No new textures, models, sounds, or third-party assets are introduced in this release.

## Advancement coverage
No new advancement is added yet. The renewable-water milestone should arrive with the actual Well rather than teaching unfinished infrastructure.

## Known limitations
Finite water does not make oceans practically easy to drain; it only removes source regeneration. Players with cheats can still alter gamerules or place water directly, as expected for administrative/Creative control.

## Validation
The release validator checks the version, `FiniteWaterSystem` registration, use of `WATER_SOURCE_CONVERSION`, and the v0.28.28-a cleanup/build protocol. The new Java system is also compiled directly against the cached Minecraft 1.21.11 and Fabric API classes.

## Next release pressure
Design and implement the Well multiblock, water containers/storage, and aquifer-yield cadence so renewable water becomes a meaningful settlement upgrade.
