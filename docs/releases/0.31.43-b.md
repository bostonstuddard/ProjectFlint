# Project Flint v0.31.43-b

## Intent
Fix remaining Utility Vest vegetation gaps, relax only the non-structural exterior cells of the Fireclay Forge, darken the Tanned Leather set, and stage the newly supplied hardwood progression art without exposing unfinished Survival progression.

## Player-facing behavior
- Wearing the Tanned Utility Vest now permits hand-clearing Project Flint forage bushes, Sweet Berry Bushes, Strawberry Bushes, and saplings in addition to its existing vegetation coverage. Project Flint forage bushes keep the one-Stick fallback.
- Tanned Leather Armor and the Tanned Utility Vest use a noticeably darker brown palette. The Utility Vest's gold chest badge is larger and brighter.
- Wood Dust, Hardwood, Hardwood Alloy, Hardwood Axe, Hardwood Hammer, Hardwood Hoe, Hardwood Pickaxe, Hardwood Shovel, and Hardwood Sword are available in the Project Flint Creative tab only. They have no Survival recipes or progression behavior yet.
- The supplied Wood Sheet artwork replaces the existing Wooden Sheet sprite; the existing Wooden Sheet gameplay item and recipes are unchanged.
- The Fireclay Forge only validates air inside its central funnel/chimney. Exterior gaps that are not specified structure blocks may contain other blocks, enabling wall-sharing and nearby construction.

## Architecture
- Forge validation checks required masonry/stairs/slabs independently from the centre-column air requirement. Unspecified exterior cells are ignored rather than claimed by the multiblock.
- The new hardwood items are inert registered Items with item definitions/models and Creative-tab exposure only. No recipes, tool capabilities, mining tiers, or processing systems were invented.
- Utility Vest vegetation permissions stay centralized in `TannedUtilityVestSystem.canHandClear`.

## Design reasoning
Only the actual hollow path through the Forge should be structurally significant; requiring every visual gap around the chimney to remain empty prevented GregTech-style shared walls and ordinary adjacent building. The hardwood asset batch is intentionally staged without balance assumptions until its progression is designed.

## Tuning snapshot
- Existing armor values, durability, Utility Vest inventory/crafting functionality, Forge timings/fuel behavior, and all current recipes are unchanged.
- No stats are assigned to the Creative-only hardwood tools yet.

## Assets and licenses
The nine new hardwood/wood-dust sprites and replacement Wooden Sheet sprite are direct user-supplied assets. Tanned armor/vest sprites are deterministic palette edits of the already-shipped Project Flint armor assets; the vest badge is a small pixel edit. No generative image asset is used.

## Advancement coverage
No advancement or Flint Quests changes. Creative-only staged items intentionally have no recipe-guide entries.

## Known limitations
The hardwood material/tool set is registration-only and Creative-only. It intentionally has no Survival acquisition or gameplay stats.

## Validation
Check Forge formation with arbitrary blocks placed in exterior corner/gap cells while keeping the centre funnel clear; then obstruct a centre funnel cell and confirm formation fails. Verify forage bushes and berry bushes can be cleared while the Utility Vest is worn, and confirm all staged hardwood items appear only through Creative access.

## Next release pressure
Define the Hardwood progression, tool statistics, and workstation recipes before exposing any of the staged items to Survival.
