# Project Flint v0.31.65-b — Mud Building and Forge Fuel

## Crafting and building
- Fireclay Forge: fixed valid Log batches not continuing automatically; new batches restart with four Logs, a usable Firestarter and space for Charcoal.
- Loose Mud Bricks: reduced the Wooden Grinder cost to eight Mud Piles. Bricks now need two Loose Mud Bricks instead of four.
- Mud: surround a Water Bucket with eight Dirt at the Hardwood Crafting Table to create eight Mud. The empty bucket is returned. One Mud makes four Mud Piles.
- Packed Mud: combine Mud and Wheat. Walking on Packed Mud increases speed by 10%.
- Mud Bricks: four Packed Mud create four Mud Bricks. Convert Mud Bricks into Loose Mud Bricks, but not back. Added Mud Brick Slabs, Stairs and Walls.
- Dirt building: added placeable Dirt Slabs and Dirt Stairs, plus Hardwood Table recipes for Loose Dirt Slabs, Loose Mud Brick Slabs, and converting Dirt to/from Loose Soil.

## Crafting balance
- Leather Straps: personal crafting now yields two; Hardwood Table yields three.
- Leather Repair Kit: personal crafting now yields one; Hardwood Table yields two.
- Clay Block: Hardwood Table consumes three Clay Balls; personal crafting still consumes four.

## Verification
Source/resource checks are provided in `tools/validate_03165.py`. Complete Gradle compilation, actual survival gameplay, and client/server multiplayer validation are separate and must not be reported as complete unless run.
