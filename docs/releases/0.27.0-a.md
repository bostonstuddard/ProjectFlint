# Project Flint v0.27.0-a

## Storage balance
- Mortar Chest capacity reduced from 7 slots to 3.
- Woven Chest capacity reduced from 12 slots to 9.

## Leather progression naming
- `Raw Hide` is now `Raw Leather` (`projectflint:raw_leather`).
- `Ground Hide` is now `Ground Leather` (`projectflint:ground_leather`).
- Leather Backpack worn rendering now uses the Ground Leather texture again while preserving the working equipment-asset registration fix.

## Grinder fixes
- Clay Grinder and Wooden Grinder now place in all four horizontal orientations.
- Both grinder GUIs now render a complete right-facing arrow, including its missing point.

## Ground objects / gravity
- Crafting piles and ground assemblies now require a sturdy supporting top face and break when that support is removed.
- Loose Dirt Slab, Crude Clay Slab, and Burnt Branch Slab now inherit falling behavior from their full-block counterparts.
- Ground assembly models now reuse their actual item textures instead of maintaining duplicate `_ground` texture variants. Vanilla Flint assemblies use `minecraft:item/flint` directly, fixing the missing Flint ground texture.

## Advanced Kiln
- Every one of the 8 Advanced Kiln multiblock blocks can now open the kiln GUI, including back and side casing blocks.
