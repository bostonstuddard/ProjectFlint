# Project Flint v0.31.36-b — Tanned Armor and Utility Vest

## Intent
Introduce a significant tanned-leather survival milestone: armor, inventory and hand-harvesting upgrades, and a restricted return of player crafting, paced behind charcoal and the Fireclay Forge.

## Player-facing behavior
- **Tanned Leather Armor:** Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots use 5/8/7/4 Tanned Leather in the Clay Grinder, respectively, with leather-equivalent appearance and equipment behavior in a darker brown. Each piece adds 0.5 armor and 96 durability to the matching vanilla leather piece.
- **Tanned Utility Vest:** Tanned Leather Chestplate + Leather Backpack with Chest combine in the Wooden Grinder (and the unlocked 2×2 personal crafting grid) into the permanent-durability Tanned Utility Vest. It has 4.5 armor, retains the chest-backpack's 10% increased exhaustion, and unlocks hotbar + first inventory row + four second-row slots (22 inventory slots total).
- When wearing the vest and using an empty hand, ordinary vegetation (grass, flowers and tall flowers, petals, leaves, sugarcane, vines, lily pads, etc.) can be broken; the Bedroll returns as one intact item, with its color retained. This privilege does not apply to wooden, stone, ore or building blocks.
- Vest restores the survival 2×2 personal crafting grid and vanilla recipe-book interface. Only recipes registered under the ProjectFlint namespace remain craftable; vanilla crafting recipes are disabled globally. Unlocked custom crafting recipes are shown in the book.
- Fireclay Forge now has two distinct output slots: finished processing outputs and charcoal fuel byproducts; existing saves migrate by preserving all old slot indices. Its future recipe output begins empty until future processing recipes are specified.
- Recipe guide no longer displays fuel as a fabricated Charcoal -> Brick Furnace recipe; Forge fuel behavior is instructional text, not a fake processing recipe. The Primitive Oven transfers half-tick timer units to avoid signed-short menu-data overflow, and both Oven and Brick Furnace show exact minute:second countdowns in addition to their burn bars. Fuel durations themselves are unchanged.
- Fireclay Forge Controller is now craftable in the Wooden Grinder from 1 Wooden Beam + 4 Fired Fireclay Bricks so players can produce Charcoal before Brick Furnace progression.
- Brick Furnace and its existing shared Brick Blast Furnace backend now accept **Charcoal only**, consuming one Charcoal per 10-minute ignition. Their fuel slot and guide text explicitly describe that fuel. Existing processing inputs/outputs and tanned-leather conversion otherwise remain untouched.

## Architecture
- Armor/vest registry and item components are in `ModItems`; Clay Grinder definitions and Wooden Grinder assembly are gameplay-truth registries. `RecipeManagerMixin` removes only `minecraft` namespace `CraftingRecipe` entries before the vanilla recipe-book sync, without touching kiln, furnace, recipe parchment, cooking or ProjectFlint recipes.
- Inventory access and temporary personal-grid slots are equipment-dependent server-side checks, with weak slot/owner tracking. Losing the vest re-locks the four added slots, with existing overflow handling.
- No ProjectFlint EMI plugin or compile-time PF-EMI bridge is added. PF-EMI stays external.

## Design reasoning
This upgrade brings back familiar Survival interaction only after the leather, water, Fireclay Forge and charcoal progression. It is not a bypass for masonry, tools, ore, or other structural gathering restrictions. Armor pieces share the leather equipment concept and retain its dyeable behavior, with a darker base coloration.

## Assets and licenses
Tanned Armor item icons and equipment layers are palette edits of vanilla Minecraft leather armor, sourced from the existing Minecraft 1.21.11 game archive; the Vest adds a small gold patch to the upper-right chest. No AI-generated asset or font files are included. All new assets are mod-namespaced and use the existing ProjectFlint visual resources.

## Advancement coverage
ProjectFlint advancements remain deliberately disabled. Vanilla's 2×2 crafting recipe book appears while wearing the Vest; only ProjectFlint-owned crafting recipe entries are shown. Flint Quests integration remains optional and unmodified.

## Known limitations
The Fireclay Forge's current charcoal-byproduct behavior is unchanged; its separate primary output slot is reserved for future processing and starts empty. No speculative metal-smelting recipes are added. The full Gradle/Loom build needs to be run with an online or populated Fabric Loom plugin cache; static checks alone do not establish a working in-game JAR.

## Validation
Run `python tools/validate_release.py`, then `build.bat`. Confirm armor attributes and durability in game; Vest equip/unequip and overflow; 2×2 book and vanilla recipe denial; empty-hand bedroll recovery; Charcoal-only 10-minute Brick Furnace fuel; Fireclay Forge controller construction. Preserve previous Well, Grinder, Oven, EMI and Quest behavior.
