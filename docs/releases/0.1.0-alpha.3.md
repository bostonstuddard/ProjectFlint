# Project Flint 0.1.0-alpha.3 — Primitive World Rules

## Intent

Alpha.3 responds directly to the first interaction-readability playtest. The goal is to make the primitive opening feel like a coherent survival ruleset instead of vanilla Minecraft with several actions cancelled after the fact.

This release establishes three long-lived assumptions:

1. carrying capacity is a progression capability;
2. the surface world should begin uncivilized and comparatively free of vanilla progression shortcuts;
3. common animals are not effortless early food.

## Player-facing behavior

### Primitive interaction

- Removed the red blocked-action crosshair marker.
- Removed the mouse-button HUD icon.
- Grass stripping no longer requires an empty main hand. Holding newly gathered Plant Fiber or another ordinary item does not strand the player from continuing the opening loop.
- Grass stripping is approximately three times slower than alpha.2: roughly 7.8 seconds of continuous breaking at the current tuning value.
- Grass progress is only shown while the attack/break control is actively held.
- Short Grass and Leaf Litter can now be cleared very slowly with an empty hand, produce no items, and apply the Mining Fatigue visual/effect while the player is attempting the wrong operation.
- Forage bushes retain the Refined Rock requirement and only show progress while actively being worked.
- Refined Rock success is increased from 55% to 62% after a completed stone-working cycle.
- Loose Rock placement frequency is increased modestly by changing the rarity filter from 1-in-5 attempts to 1-in-4 attempts.

### Carrying and crafting

- Survival carrying capacity is limited to hotbar slots 1–5.
- Hotbar slots 6–9 and all 27 main-inventory storage slots are server-enforced as unavailable.
- The offhand remains available because the current stone-working interaction uses one Rock in each hand.
- If an item reaches a locked storage slot through an unexpected vanilla path, Project Flint attempts to move it into the five legal slots and otherwise drops the remainder instead of silently deleting it.
- The personal 2x2 crafting area is disabled and visually removed from the survival inventory screen.
- The survival inventory recipe-book button is hidden and its toggle is disabled.
- Creative mode keeps normal inventory access for development/testing.

### Animals and threats

- Ordinary passive animals receive a permanent +100% max-health modifier when loaded, effectively doubling their health without repeatedly stacking the modifier.
- Retaliating animals gain another 0.5 blocks of contact-attack reach compared with alpha.2.
- Striking an animal alerts nearby living animals of the same species within 16 blocks, causing a local herd/group retaliation response.
- Fish are temporarily immune to damage. Their eventual acquisition method belongs to a later food/fishing redesign rather than the primitive hand-combat loop.
- Untamed vanilla wolves actively seek survival players within 36 blocks instead of waiting to be provoked.
- Surface hostile mobs are removed in the Overworld when they load at/near the motion-blocking surface or with direct sky access. Underground hostiles are intentionally retained for later cave progression work.
- Wandering Traders, Trader Llamas, and Pillagers are removed from the primitive Overworld when loaded.

### Surface world

New Overworld generation suppresses vanilla surface/civilization structures that shortcut or contradict the intended opening world, including:

- villages;
- pillager outposts;
- desert and jungle pyramids;
- swamp huts and igloos;
- woodland mansions;
- shipwrecks;
- ocean ruins and monuments;
- buried treasure;
- trail ruins;
- ruined portals.

Underground structures such as mineshafts, strongholds, ancient cities, and trial chambers are intentionally not removed by this pass. They will be evaluated when underground progression is designed.

### Progression feedback

All `minecraft` namespace advancements are filtered out during server advancement loading. Alpha.3 introduces the first Project Flint advancement path:

- **Stone in Hand** — obtain a Rock;
- **A Better Edge** — obtain a Refined Rock;
- **Disturbed Earth** — obtain Plant Fiber;
- **First Timber** — obtain a Twig.

These are milestones, not arbitrary unlock buttons. The mechanics themselves remain capability-driven.

## Architecture introduced

### PrimitiveInventorySystem

`PrimitiveInventorySystem` owns opening-game carrying capacity. It defines the five legal storage slots, repairs illegal inventory state once per server tick, and provides a shared insertion method so Project Flint rewards respect primitive capacity rather than calling vanilla inventory insertion directly.

`SlotMixin` denies placement/pickup through locked player slots. `InventoryMenuMixin` marks the personal crafting slots as unavailable. `InventoryScreenMixin` is client presentation only: it hides the recipe-book entry point and makes unavailable storage obvious.

### PassiveMobTuningSystem

Passive-animal durability is implemented as a named permanent `MAX_HEALTH` attribute modifier (`projectflint:primitive_passive_health`). A stable identifier makes the modifier idempotent: loading an animal repeatedly cannot double it again and again.

### PrimitiveWorldRulesSystem

This system contains temporary broad world/ecology gates that do not warrant species-specific systems yet: fish damage immunity, removal of selected civilization NPCs, and removal of surface-loaded hostile mobs.

### WildWolfAggressionSystem

Wild-wolf hostility is separated from generic retaliation. It periodically selects nearby survival players for untamed wolves and gives them an active target/navigation order.

### AnimalRetaliationSystem group response

Retaliation is now seeded to nearby same-type animals rather than only the entity directly struck. This establishes the beginning of herd/group behavior without pretending the current generic steering is the final animal-AI system.

### World-generation interception

`ChunkGeneratorMixin` intercepts vanilla `tryGenerateStructure` in the Overworld and rejects a documented set of surface/civilization structure IDs. This is deliberately centralized so the removed set is reviewable rather than scattered among structure-specific hacks.

### Advancement filtering

`ServerAdvancementManagerMixin` filters the reload input so vanilla advancements never enter the server advancement tree. Project Flint advancements remain ordinary data resources under the mod namespace.

## Design reasoning

### Five slots are a capability, not punishment

A primitive character should not begin with thirty-six abstract item-storage slots. Five hotbar slots create immediate choices about what can be carried and make future storage technology meaningful. The restriction is deliberately centralized so future bags, baskets, packs, containers, or other carrying upgrades can expand capacity through one system instead of fighting dozens of GUI patches.

### Offhand remains available

The current opening loop already communicates two-object manipulation by requiring a Rock in each hand. Locking the offhand merely to achieve a mathematically smaller inventory would break an existing physical interaction and would not improve the design.

### Short vegetation is removable but not harvestable

Short Grass and Leaf Litter should not be permanent collisionless decorations the player can never clear, but bare hands should not turn them into free resources. Slow clearing plus Mining Fatigue says: *you can force this out of the way, but you do not yet possess the correct operation for useful harvesting.*

### The surface starts uncivilized

Villages, traders, temples, wrecks, portals, and similar vanilla content provide finished materials, loot, shelter, and technological implications that bypass the literal primitive premise. Removing them from new surface generation gives Project Flint room to introduce human infrastructure only when the player or future world systems have earned it.

Underground content is not being erased blindly. It remains until the cave/mining progression has enough design context to decide what should survive, be replaced, or become inaccessible.

### Animals should change food decisions

Early meat should not be the obvious answer to hunger simply because Minecraft animals normally offer little resistance. Doubling health, increasing reach, and adding same-species group retaliation makes attacking a herd a commitment. Species-specific behavior remains the intended destination; alpha.3 is an enforceable baseline.

### Temporary art should be useful, not embarrassing

Testing requires readable art before final art exists. Alpha.3 replaces the first crude placeholders with new Project Flint-owned 16x16 temporary sprites and five biome-oriented bush families. No third-party texture pixels are included. External permissive packs remain references for silhouette/readability research only.

## Assets and licensing

Alpha.3 redistributes **no community resource-pack textures**.

All changed temporary textures are original Project Flint development placeholders created specifically for this release. They are listed in `docs/TEMPORARY_ASSETS.md` and are expected to be replaced by final user-created art.

External Modrinth packs are maintained in `docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md` only as licensing/style research. In particular, permissively licensed vegetation projects can inform silhouette and density, while ARR material remains explicitly excluded from the repository.

Custom block models now define explicit particle textures. This fixes the NULL/error particle appearance seen when breaking Project Flint blocks.

## Known limitations

- Existing structures in already-generated chunks are not removed. A fresh world is required to judge the alpha.3 surface-generation rules.
- Surface hostile suppression currently discards matching entities after they load. It achieves the gameplay rule but should later move closer to spawn-selection logic if profiling shows repeated spawn attempts are wasteful.
- Passive health tuning currently targets vanilla natural passive spawn categories (creature, ambient, axolotl, and aquatic groups) while deliberately excluding `MISC` entities such as players/utility entities.
- Wild wolves receive active targets/navigation; their visual vanilla anger state is not separately rewritten in this release.
- Herd retaliation is generic and makes no distinction between species temperament.
- Five-slot inventory visuals are intentionally functional placeholders, not final inventory art.
- Project Flint advancement presentation is minimal and will need a coherent custom advancement background/icon language.
- Old alpha worlds may contain previously generated shore bushes and now-removed vanilla structures.

## Next pressure

The next development slice should build forward from the now-constrained opening rather than add more arbitrary prohibitions:

1. formalize an operation/tool-capability API for cutting, scraping, digging, splitting, and related actions;
2. give primitive vegetation/material gathering distinct outputs rather than generic placeholders;
3. begin first composite-material progression and real portable storage progression;
4. replace generic animal retaliation with initial species behavior profiles;
5. move more balance/content definitions into reloadable Project Flint data so playtest changes require fewer Java rebuilds;
6. continue replacing temporary visuals from the tracked asset ledger.
