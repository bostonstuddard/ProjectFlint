# Project Flint v0.31.34-b — Workstation Models and Fireclay Forge

## Intent
Repair three Creative-only workstation visuals and broaden the existing fireclay progression with a Creative-only structural charcoal forge, while improving Nest, Bucket and Well Pulley clarity and crafting cost.

## Player-facing behavior
- Nest recipe in Wooden Grinder: 16 Plant Fiber and 2 vanilla String -> 1 Nest.
- Well Pulley recipe in Wooden Grinder: 16 of any stripped overworld or Nether log/wood/stem/hyphae and 1 Rope -> 1 Pulley; the casing has a stone rim, timber core, and a dark rope hole on its underside.
- Clay Bucket (Water) and Clay Bucket (Milk) use matching naming. Milk Bucket cannot be consumed as food. Milk still goes into its Wooden Grinder cup conversion, returning the empty Clay Bucket.
- Basin and Millstone now have element-shaped models made with the respective owner-supplied texture atlas. Drying Rack retains the owner-supplied Blockbench model geometry with corrected ProjectFlint texture paths. All remain Creative-only, without recipes or processing inventories.
- Creative-only Fireclay Forge Controller can face four horizontal directions. Its front indicator and GUI report whether its brick shell is formed and whether it is lit. No crafting recipe is registered.
- Forge inventory: input overworld logs/wood, a Flint/Bone/Wood Firestarter, output Charcoal. A successful Ignite button click uses one durability, reserves the required input, and begins a 45-second recipe. With additional valid input and durability, every completed batch automatically ignites the next one and consumes an additional durability. Work and prepaid materials persist on reload. If output is full, output is retained without losses.
- Burning Forge emits quiet furnace-like crackling, casts light level 11 from the controller, and emits smoke at the raised chimney opening.

## Architecture
Forge gameplay is ProjectFlint-owned: `FireclayForgeMultiblock`, `FireclayForgeControllerBlock`, `FireclayForgeBlockEntity`, `FireclayForgeMenu` and `FireclayForgeScreen`. PF-EMI remains a separate external runtime mod. No ProjectFlint EMI source, entrypoint or recipe registry is added. The Forge controller is Creative-only until a later progression recipe is specified.

## Design reasoning
A manually activated first batch prevents an unattended forge from operating on placement. Subsequent batches repeat the physical ignition cost, using existing Firestarter capability rules. Reserved inputs live in a hidden persistent slot rather than being deleted if structure is dismantled. Ordinary fireclay blocks are not converted or claimed, so two separately valid structures may share suitable casing/walls.

## Tuning snapshot
- 3x3 footprint, solid nine-block floor, two hollow masonry rings; front-center lower ring is the Controller, upper rear-center raised chimney brick, interior/mouth must be dry and open or replaceable. Controller is one block above the base and points outward.
- Input: 4 overworld log/wood blocks, stripped or unstripped (vanilla `logs_that_burn` tag); output: 4 vanilla Charcoal; cycle: 900 ticks / 45 seconds.
- 1 Firestarter durability per batch, including automatic relights; no starter/tool, missing input, invalid casing or full output halts future cycles. One starter may be consumed/broken on its final valid ignition.
- Lit controller emits block light 11; crackling radius is modest (0.22 sound volume at controller).

## Assets and licenses
- Reuses unmodified owner-supplied stitched Basin, Millstone and Drying Rack texture atlases. Drying Rack Blockbench element geometry was preserved with a `projectflint` namespace correction. Basin/Millstone element geometry was newly authored over these atlases, instead of applying `cube_all`.
- Three pulley textures and three controller-front state textures are project-specific derived pixel composites of owner-supplied fireclay/station textures, NOT byte-identical direct uploads. Creation and final hashes are recorded in `docs/texture_provenance.json` and the appendix of `docs/TEXTURE_PROVENANCE.md`.

## Advancement coverage
Legacy vanilla/ProjectFlint advancements remain retired; Flint Quests compatibility unchanged. New recipes are in the Wooden Grinder registry and therefore appear in ProjectFlint's own grinder guide. Forge recipe/UI is Creative-only; recipe-discovery implementation is left entirely to external PF-EMI.

## Known limitations
Forge artwork is a code/asset prototype; final layout and charcoal ratios may be tuned later. A full Gradle/Loom build and in-game rendering test require the user's local environment. No new workstation processing system is invented for Basin/Drying Rack/Millstone.

## Validation
Run the updated source/release validator, verify model and blockstate JSON, source-diff audit and patch-over-v0.31.33-b simulation. A failed offline Loom resolution is not a successful Gradle compile.
